# Context Recovery Protocol

**Version:** v0.1 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document records the operational protocol developed for recovering interrupted long-duration Human–AI research dialogues.

The objective is not to restore system memory.

Instead, the protocol enables the recovery of research continuity after a dialogue reaches the context limit.

The protocol emerged through operational experience and is documented as part of the Research Program's Operational Development.

---

# Background

As the Research Program evolved, dialogue sessions frequently extended over many hours and produced large volumes of research observations.

In some cases, the conversation reached the maximum context length before the planned final deliverable could be generated.

Without an explicit recovery procedure, valuable reasoning processes and operational conclusions could be lost despite the dialogue itself having already reached a stable endpoint.

This operational challenge motivated the development of a Context Recovery Protocol.

---

# Observed Problem

The interruption did not occur during exploration.

Instead, it occurred immediately before externalization of the final research asset.

The operational sequence became:

Long-duration Dialogue

↓

Completed Reasoning

↓

Context Limit

↓

Final Deliverable Interrupted

The problem therefore concerned operational continuity rather than reasoning quality.

---

# Recovery Principle

The protocol assumes that the dialogue itself has already completed its reasoning process.

The objective is not to restart discussion.

Instead, the objective is to reconstruct the final deliverable naturally emerging from the completed dialogue.

Recovery therefore prioritizes continuity over re-exploration.

# Operational Procedure

The following operational sequence proved effective.

1. Preserve the complete dialogue as a dialogue archive.
2. Begin a new conversation.
3. Initialize the dialogue in Recovery Mode.
4. Provide the archived dialogue as primary evidence.
5. Explicitly define the mission as recovery rather than exploration.
6. Reconstruct only the intended final deliverable.

This procedure minimizes unnecessary re-analysis while preserving the conclusions already reached.

---

# Operational Characteristics

Unlike ordinary summarization, Context Recovery treats the archived dialogue as primary research evidence.

The AI is instructed not to generate new hypotheses.

Instead, it reconstructs the research asset that would naturally have been produced had the original dialogue not been interrupted.

This distinction preserves methodological continuity.

# Initial Observation

This protocol was developed through practical operation rather than theoretical planning.

Its successful application suggests that dialogue interruption does not necessarily terminate research continuity, provided that:

- the dialogue archive is preserved;
- the recovery objective is clearly defined;
- recovery is distinguished from renewed exploration.

Further operational experience will determine the generality of this protocol.
