# Research Entry Architecture Emergence

**Architecture Status:** Emerging Operational Concept (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document records the operational observations that led to the emergence of the concept tentatively referred to as **Research Entry Architecture**.

The purpose of this document is not to establish a new architectural layer.

Instead, it preserves the reasoning process through which a previously unrecognized design target gradually became visible during practical Research Program operation.

---

# Background

During preparation of the 12P Endorsement workflow, the Research Experience Designer was asked to improve the initial experience of external researchers visiting the Research Program.

The original objective was relatively limited.

The task was expected to improve repository navigation and reduce unnecessary cognitive burden for first-time visitors.

No new architectural concept was anticipated at the beginning of the task.

---

# Operational Observation

As the design process progressed, repeated observations suggested that the primary design target was not the repository itself.

Instead, the central problem appeared to be the relationship between:

Research Asset

↓

Specific Audience

↓

Initial Entry Point

The design activity therefore shifted from repository navigation toward audience-oriented research entry.

This transition emerged gradually through observation rather than prior theoretical planning.

---

# Emerging Observation

The operational discussions suggested that different audiences require different entry interfaces.

For example:

12P

↓

Endorsement Candidate

↓

12P First Contact

Rather than providing a single universal repository entrance, the design process indicated that entry interfaces may depend upon the combination of:

- Research Asset
- Intended Audience
- Communication Objective

At the present stage, this observation remains an operational hypothesis.

---

# Current Interpretation

The present observations suggest the possible emergence of a distinct design target.

Tentatively, this target may be described as:

**Research Entry Architecture**

This concept refers to the organization of initial research entry experiences designed for specific audiences rather than the internal organization of research repositories themselves.

Whether this concept represents an independent architectural layer remains an open research question.

---

# Current Status

Current evidence is based primarily upon one operational case:

- 12P First Contact

Although the observations are internally consistent, they have not yet been validated across multiple research assets.

Generalization is therefore intentionally deferred.

---

# Future Validation

Future operational studies may examine whether similar entry structures emerge for additional research assets, including:

- Research Architecture
- Formation History
- Presentation Audience
- Operational Architecture
- Comparative Observation

If comparable organizational patterns are repeatedly observed, the concept may later be reconsidered as a candidate architectural layer.
