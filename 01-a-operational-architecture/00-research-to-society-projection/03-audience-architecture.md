# Audience Architecture

**Architecture Version:** v1.0 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the audience architecture used within the Research-to-Society Projection Framework.

The objective is not simply to classify people into categories.

Instead, the architecture identifies different modes of engagement through which completed Research Packages may be projected.

Different audiences require different communication strategies, while the underlying research remains identical.

---

# Audience-independent Research

Research itself is independent of audience.

Completed Research Packages remain unchanged regardless of who accesses them.

Audience adaptation begins only after a Research Package enters the Projection Layer.

This separation preserves methodological integrity throughout the communication process.

---

# Audience Architecture

The Bridge Layer recognizes multiple communication domains.

These domains represent different communication objectives rather than different versions of the research.

---

## Research Audience

Primary objective:

Scientific understanding.

Typical communication includes:

- research papers
- methodologies
- observation records
- comparative frameworks

Research audiences prioritize methodological rigor and theoretical consistency.

---

## Enterprise Audience

Primary objective:

Practical organizational understanding.

Communication emphasizes:

- operational value
- organizational application
- collaboration opportunities
- dialogue demonstrations

Research remains unchanged while communication becomes operationally oriented.

---

## Educational Audience

Primary objective:

Learning and knowledge development.

Communication focuses on:

- conceptual understanding
- progressive explanation
- visual learning
- structured educational resources

Educational projection supports learning without altering research concepts.

---

## Professional Audience

Primary objective:

Practical implementation.

Examples include:

- AI practitioners
- consultants
- knowledge management specialists
- system designers

Projection emphasizes implementation pathways rather than theoretical discussion.

---

## Community Audience

Primary objective:

Shared exploration.

Communication encourages discussion, collaborative interpretation, and gradual understanding of the Research Program.

---

## Public Audience

Primary objective:

Initial accessibility.

Communication introduces fundamental ideas through approachable narratives, visual explanations, and introductory materials.

Public projection serves as an entry point rather than a complete representation of the research.

---

# Audience Relationships

The audiences do not form a hierarchy.

Instead, they represent different perspectives from which the same Research Package may be approached.

Research Package

↓

Projection Layer

↓

Research Audience

Enterprise Audience

Educational Audience

Professional Audience

Community Audience

Public Audience

Each projection preserves the same underlying research while adapting communication to the needs of the intended audience.

---

# Audience Transition

Audiences may move gradually between domains.

For example:

Public

↓

Educational

↓

Professional

↓

Research

or

Enterprise

↓

Research Collaboration

↓

Joint Research

The Projection Architecture therefore supports continuous engagement rather than isolated communication events.

---

# Audience Independence

The Bridge Layer never creates different versions of the research.

Instead, different audiences receive different communication pathways toward the same Research Package.

The architecture therefore separates:

Research

↓

Projection

↓

Audience

rather than

Research

↓

Different Research Versions

---

# Relationship to Projection Framework

The Audience Architecture operates after the Research Package Projection Framework.

Research Packages define what is projected.

Audience Architecture defines who receives the projection.

Subsequent documents define how projection is performed and which media are assigned.

---

# Summary

Audience Architecture establishes the communication domains through which Research Packages are projected into society.

By separating research from audience adaptation, the Bridge Layer preserves methodological integrity while enabling diverse forms of communication across different social contexts.
