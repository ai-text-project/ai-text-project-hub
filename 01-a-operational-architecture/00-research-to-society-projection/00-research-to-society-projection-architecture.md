# Research-to-Society Projection Architecture

**Architecture Version:** v1.0 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the overall architecture through which completed research assets are projected from the Research Program into broader social communication.

The objective is not to simplify research or translate individual documents.

Instead, the architecture preserves the structural integrity and developmental continuity of the Research Program while enabling different audiences to access the research through appropriate forms of communication.

The architecture therefore serves as the upper-level operational layer connecting the Research Space and society.

---

# Background

The Dialogue-Phase Reasoning Research Program has produced a growing collection of completed research assets.

These assets include research papers, methodologies, presentation packages, executive summaries, visual architectures, dialogue demonstrations, GitHub repositories, and supporting research documentation.

Until now, most of these assets have primarily addressed research-oriented audiences.

As the Research Program continues to mature, a new architectural layer becomes necessary.

Rather than producing individual communication materials directly, this layer coordinates how completed research assets should be projected toward different audiences while preserving methodological consistency.

This layer is referred to as the Research-to-Society Projection Architecture.

---

# Architectural Position

The Projection Architecture operates above the Research Space.

The Research Space preserves and organizes completed research assets.

The Projection Architecture determines how these assets should be communicated to society.

The architecture therefore separates:

- Research preservation
- Research navigation
- Research projection

into independent but connected operational layers.

---

# Fundamental Philosophy

The Projection Architecture is based on the following principles.

- One Research
- Multiple Projections
- Shared Methodological Integrity

Research itself remains unchanged.

Only the method of communication is adapted according to audience, communication objectives, and media characteristics.

The architecture therefore treats communication as projection rather than translation.

---

# Overall Architecture

The operational flow can be summarized as follows.

Research Program

↓

Research Space

↓

Navigation Layer

↓

Projection Layer

↓

Audience-specific Communication

↓

Social Observation

↓

Research Program

This architecture establishes a continuous cycle connecting research development and social communication.

---

# Scope

The Projection Architecture covers the design of:

- Research Package Projection
- Audience Architecture
- Navigation and Projection Layers
- Phase Transformation Strategy
- Media Assignment
- Production Workflow
- Role Ecosystem
- Governance

Individual communication products such as Medium articles, X posts, presentations, or educational materials are developed separately according to this architecture.

---

# Position within the Operational Architecture

This document serves as the master architecture for the Research-to-Society Projection domain.

The detailed operational components are described in the subsequent documents contained within this directory.

Together, these documents establish a coherent Bridge Layer that connects completed research assets with society while preserving the structural identity of the Research Program.

---

# Note

The architecture is motivated not only by the need to organize research assets, but also by the need to establish sustainable pathways through which society can discover, understand, and engage with those assets. Structural organization alone does not guarantee meaningful social connection. The Projection Architecture therefore addresses the design of those connections.
