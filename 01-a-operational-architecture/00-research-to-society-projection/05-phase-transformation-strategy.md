# Phase Transformation Strategy

**Strategy Version:** v1.0 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the strategic role of Phase Transformation within the Research-to-Society Projection Architecture.

The objective is not to translate research into simpler language.

Instead, the objective is to transform completed Research Packages into communication forms that are appropriate for different audiences while preserving methodological integrity.

Phase Transformation therefore represents a change in communication rather than a change in research.

---

# Position within the Projection Architecture

Phase Transformation operates after the Projection Layer has identified:

- the Research Package
- the intended audience
- the communication objective
- the destination media

The transformation process begins only after these strategic decisions have been completed.

# Transformation Philosophy

Phase Transformation is based on a single fundamental principle.

**Transformation is not Translation.**

Translation changes language.

Phase Transformation changes communication.

The objective is to preserve the research while redesigning its communication according to audience, communication purpose, and media characteristics.

---

# Scope of Transformation

Phase Transformation may include:

- structural transformation
- narrative transformation
- visual transformation
- communication-density adjustment
- sequencing of information
- audience-oriented explanation

The research itself remains unchanged throughout the process.

---

# Elements that must be preserved

Every transformation should preserve:

- conceptual relationships
- methodological consistency
- logical structure
- developmental continuity
- research identity

These elements define the structural integrity of the Research Package.

---

# Elements that may change

Transformation may adapt:

- communication sequence
- narrative style
- visual presentation
- terminology density
- explanation depth
- communication medium

These adaptations improve accessibility without altering the research.

# Relationship to Media Assignment

Media Assignment determines where communication will occur.

Phase Transformation determines how communication should change before production begins.

These responsibilities remain intentionally separated.

---

# Relationship to Production Roles

The output of Phase Transformation is not a finished communication product.

Instead, it becomes a production specification for downstream roles.

Typical recipients include:

- Bridge Content Producer
- Visual Designer
- Presentation Designer
- Dialogue Designer

Production roles execute the transformation strategy without redefining it.

---

# Future Expansion

As new communication media emerge, additional transformation strategies may be developed.

Because the Bridge Layer separates Projection from Production, future media can be supported without modifying completed Research Packages.

This architecture therefore supports continuous expansion while preserving research consistency.

---

# Summary

Phase Transformation serves as the strategic bridge between Projection Architecture and communication production.

It transforms communication rather than research.

By preserving structural integrity while adapting communication, the Bridge Layer enables one Research Package to support multiple forms of social engagement without compromising methodological identity.
