# Purpose

This document provides an architectural overview of the Research-to-Society Projection Architecture.

Rather than defining individual operational components, this document illustrates how the architecture is organized as an integrated operational system.

The map serves as the primary entry point for understanding the relationships among the architectural documents.

---

# Architectural Overview

The Research-to-Society Projection Architecture is organized into four operational layers.

Research Foundation

Each layer builds upon the previous one while maintaining clear separation of responsibilities.

# Summary
