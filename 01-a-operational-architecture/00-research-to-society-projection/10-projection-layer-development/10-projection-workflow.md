# Projection Workflow

**Architecture Version:** v0.1 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the operational workflow through which research assets are projected from the Research Space toward public communication.

The objective of this workflow is not to produce communication products directly, but to define the architectural pathway that connects research assets to subsequent Phase Transformation.

Within the current scope of the Research-to-Society Projection Architecture, the target communication media are:

- Medium Articles
- X Posts

---

# Position within the Research Architecture

The Projection Workflow is positioned between the Research Space and the Phase Transformation process.

```text
Research Space
      │
      ▼
Research Package
      │
      ▼
Projection Unit
      │
      ▼
Phase Transformation
      │
      ├──► Medium Article
      │
      └──► X Post
```

This workflow defines how research assets leave the Research Space and become available for communication-oriented transformation.

---

# Operational Flow

The Projection Workflow consists of the following stages.

## Stage 1

Research Package

Research assets are prepared within the Research Space.

Examples include:

- Papers
- Methodologies
- Figures
- Executive Summaries
- Research Notes

These assets remain unchanged within the Research Space.

---

## Stage 2

Projection Unit

A Projection Unit is prepared from one or more Research Packages.

The Projection Unit serves as the architectural connection between research assets and the subsequent transformation process.

---

## Stage 3

Phase Transformation

The Projection Unit is transferred to the Phase Transformation process.

This process adapts the research content into communication suitable for the target medium.

Within the current architecture, the supported media are:

- Medium Articles
- X Posts

---

# Role Boundary

Within this workflow, the Bridge-Layer Strategist is responsible for:

- Understanding the Research Package
- Preparing the Projection Unit
- Transferring the Projection Unit to the Phase Transformation process

The production of Medium Articles and X Posts belongs to subsequent operational roles and is outside the responsibility of the Bridge-Layer Strategist.

---

# Summary

The Projection Workflow defines the operational pathway that connects research assets to public communication.

Its primary purpose is to establish a consistent architectural transition from Research Packages to Phase Transformation while preserving the structural integrity of the Research Space.
