# Medium Projection Guideline

**Architecture Version:** v0.1 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the basic guideline for projecting research assets into Medium Articles within the Research-to-Society Projection Architecture.

The objective is to preserve the structural integrity of the Research Program while adapting research assets into article-oriented communication.

---

# Position within the Projection Workflow

```text
Research Package
      │
      ▼
Projection Unit
      │
      ▼
Phase Transformation
      │
      ▼
Medium Article
```

This document applies after the Projection Unit has been transferred to the Phase Transformation process.

---

# Guiding Principles

Medium Articles should preserve the developmental continuity of the original Research Package.

Projection should emphasize explanation rather than simplification.

The objective is to communicate the research architecture clearly while maintaining consistency with the original research assets.

---

# Projection Objectives

Medium projection should:

- introduce the research topic clearly,
- explain the architectural background,
- present representative research assets,
- maintain logical continuity throughout the article,
- encourage readers to continue exploring the Research Program.

---

# Scope

This guideline applies only to Medium Articles.

Projection into other communication media is defined separately.
