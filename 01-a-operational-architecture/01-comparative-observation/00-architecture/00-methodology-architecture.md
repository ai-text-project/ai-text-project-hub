# Comparative Observation Methodology Architecture

**Architecture Version:** v0.1 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the overall architecture of the Comparative Observation Methodology.

Rather than describing individual observation procedures, this document explains how the methodology is organized as a complete operational system.

The objective is to establish a stable architectural foundation before developing individual methodological components.

---

# Position within the Research Program

The Comparative Observation Methodology is one of the operational methodologies that support the Research Program.

Unlike methodologies that focus on producing a specific research asset, this methodology focuses on observing, comparing, and interpreting external research resources through a consistent structural framework.

Its primary objective is to transform multiple independent observations into coherent comparative knowledge.

---

# Design Philosophy

The methodology is based on the following architectural principles.

- Architecture before implementation
- Structural comparison rather than content comparison
- Comparative interpretation rather than evaluation
- Progressive methodological development
- Reusable operational components

The methodology therefore separates architectural design, principles, operational procedures, and research production into independent but connected layers.

---

Each layer has an independent responsibility while contributing to the overall comparative research process.

---

# Layer Overview

## Architecture

Defines the overall structure of the methodology.

Focus:

- methodology structure
- development roadmap
- architectural consistency

---

## Principles

Defines the fundamental principles that govern comparative observation.

Examples include:

- structural correspondence
- comparison unit definition
- observation boundaries

---

## Framework

Defines the comparative dimensions through which external resources are interpreted.

The framework provides a stable analytical perspective that remains independent of individual transcripts or papers.

---

## Selection

Defines how observation targets are selected.

Selection focuses on structural relevance rather than popularity or technical performance.

---

## Observation

Defines the operational procedures for conducting comparative observations.

This layer standardizes observation records while preserving flexibility for different research resources.

---

## Interpretation

Defines how observations are transformed into structural interpretations.

Interpretation emphasizes recurring structures rather than isolated findings.

---

## Production

Defines how comparative observations contribute to research assets.

Typical outputs include:

- Comparative Research Skeleton
- Comparative Research Notes
- Comparative Papers

---

# Development Strategy

The methodology is expected to evolve incrementally.

Rather than designing every component in advance, each layer may mature through practical comparative observation activities.

This document therefore represents an architectural foundation rather than a completed methodology.
