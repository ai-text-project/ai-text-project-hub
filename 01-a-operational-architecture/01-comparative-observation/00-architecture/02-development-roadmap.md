# Comparative Observation Methodology Development Roadmap

**Architecture Version:** v0.1 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the planned developmental process of the Comparative Observation Methodology.

Unlike the Methodology Architecture, which describes the structural organization of the methodology, the Development Roadmap explains how the methodology is expected to mature through practical comparative observation activities.

The objective is to preserve the developmental continuity of the methodology while allowing incremental refinement.

---

# Development Philosophy

The Comparative Observation Methodology is not designed as a complete system from the beginning.

Instead, it is expected to evolve through repeated comparative observation, practical experimentation, and continuous refinement.

Each development stage produces operational assets that become the foundation for subsequent stages.

# Incremental Development Strategy

The methodology is expected to evolve incrementally.

Future versions may introduce:

- additional comparative dimensions
- new observation targets
- improved operational procedures
- expanded research production assets

without altering the fundamental architectural structure.

---

# Formation History

The methodology itself is regarded as a research asset.

Its developmental history should therefore be documented alongside its operational assets.

Changes to architectural components are expected to be recorded through successive version updates rather than replacing previous developmental stages.
