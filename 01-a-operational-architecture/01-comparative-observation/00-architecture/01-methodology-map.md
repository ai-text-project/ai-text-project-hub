The map serves as a navigation guide for the methodology.

# Position within the Operational Architecture

The Comparative Observation Methodology belongs to the Operational Architecture of the Research Program.

Its primary role is to provide a repeatable process for transforming independent research resources into structured comparative knowledge.

Unlike individual research papers, this methodology focuses on maintaining operational consistency across multiple comparative studies.
