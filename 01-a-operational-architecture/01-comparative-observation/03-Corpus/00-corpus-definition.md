# Corpus Definition

**Architecture Version:** v0.1 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the concept of a Corpus within the Comparative Observation Methodology.

The Corpus represents the collection of research resources that serve as candidates for comparative observation.

Rather than functioning as a simple repository of documents, the Corpus provides the observational foundation from which structural comparison is performed.

---

The Corpus layer follows the Comparative Framework.

The Framework defines how observation is performed.

The Corpus defines what may be observed.

---

# Definition

A Corpus is a managed collection of research resources prepared for comparative observation.

Each resource contributes observational evidence that may reveal structural correspondence across multiple domains.

The Corpus therefore functions as the observational input layer of the methodology.

---

# Characteristics of a Corpus

A Corpus should satisfy the following characteristics.

## Diversity

Resources may originate from different disciplines, research traditions, or communication media.

Examples include:

- scientific papers
- research presentations
- interview transcripts
- educational videos
- technical repositories
- operational documents

Diversity increases the opportunity to discover recurring structural organizations.

---

## Structural Relevance

Resources are selected because they are expected to contain observable structural organizations.

Selection is therefore based upon structural relevance rather than topical similarity alone.

---

## Independence

Each resource remains independent.

The methodology does not assume that resources share terminology, theoretical assumptions, or conclusions.

Comparative observation seeks structural correspondence across independently developed resources.

---

## Reusability

Once incorporated into the Corpus, a research resource may be reused across multiple comparative observations.

The Corpus is therefore regarded as a continuously evolving research asset.

---

# Relationship with the Comparative Framework

The Comparative Framework provides analytical dimensions.

The Corpus provides observational materials.

Accordingly,

```
Comparative Framework

        │

        ▼

Corpus

        │

        ▼

Comparative Observation
```

represents the transition from analytical design to operational observation.

---

# Relationship with Observation

Not every resource contained within the Corpus will necessarily be observed.

Instead,

```
Corpus

        │

        ▼

Selection

        │

        ▼

Comparative Observation
```

defines the operational workflow.

The Selection process identifies the most appropriate resources for a particular comparative study.

---

# Future Development

As the Research Program expands, the Corpus is expected to include additional categories of research resources.

The methodology is therefore designed to accommodate continuous Corpus growth while preserving methodological consistency.
