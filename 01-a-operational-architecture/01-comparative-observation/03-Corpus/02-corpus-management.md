# Corpus Management

**Architecture Version:** v0.1 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the operational management of the Comparative Observation Corpus.

The objective is to maintain a reusable and continuously evolving collection of research resources while preserving methodological consistency across comparative observations.

Corpus Management ensures that research resources remain organized, traceable, and reusable throughout the Research Program.

---

# Management Objectives

Corpus Management pursues four primary objectives.

- Maintain reusable research resources.
- Preserve traceability across comparative observations.
- Support continuous corpus expansion.
- Ensure consistent resource organization.

---

# Corpus Lifecycle

Each research resource progresses through the following lifecycle.

```
Resource Discovery

        │

        ▼

Resource Registration

        │

        ▼

Resource Classification

        │

        ▼

Comparative Observation

        │

        ▼

Interpretation

        │

        ▼

```

A resource may participate in multiple comparative observations throughout its lifecycle.

---

# Resource Metadata

Each resource should maintain basic management information.

Recommended metadata include:

- Resource ID
- Resource Title
- Resource Category
- Source
- Observation Status
- Observation History
- Related Comparative Studies
- Notes

Metadata supports long-term traceability rather than evaluation.

---

# Observation Status

Each resource may progress through several operational states.

Examples include:

- Registered
- Under Review
- Selected
- Observed
- Interpreted
- Archived

These states describe operational progress rather than research quality.

---

# Resource Reuse

Research resources are regarded as reusable assets.

A single transcript, paper, or presentation may contribute to multiple comparative observations conducted at different times.

Consequently, resources are never regarded as consumed after one observation.

Instead, each observation enriches the overall understanding of the resource.

---

# Corpus Expansion

The Corpus is expected to grow continuously.

New resources should be incorporated when they:

- introduce new structural perspectives;
- complement existing observations;
- strengthen comparative interpretation; or
- broaden methodological applicability.

Expansion should prioritize structural diversity rather than numerical size.

---

# Quality Management

Corpus quality is maintained through methodological consistency.

Priority is given to:

- structural relevance;
- diversity of perspectives;
- traceable resource history;
- reusability.

Quality is therefore determined by observational value rather than publication status.

---

# Relationship with Observation

Corpus Management prepares research resources for observation.

```
Corpus Management

        │

        ▼

Selection

        │

        ▼

Comparative Observation
```

Selection is therefore regarded as an operational process built upon managed research resources.

---

# Future Development

Future versions may introduce:

- corpus indexing;
- observation history visualization;
- comparative relationship mapping;
- automated metadata management.

These enhancements should preserve compatibility with the existing Corpus architecture.
