# Resource Classification

**Architecture Version:** v0.1 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the classification of research resources managed within the Comparative Observation Corpus.

Rather than organizing resources according to academic disciplines alone, the Comparative Observation Methodology classifies resources according to their role in structural observation.

The objective is to establish a consistent resource organization that supports reusable comparative observation.

---

# Classification Principle

Research resources are classified according to their observational role rather than their publication format.

Accordingly, different media may belong to the same resource category if they contribute similar structural evidence.

---

# Resource Categories

## Category A

### Research Literature

Examples

- Research Papers
- Technical Reports
- Preprints
- Academic Articles

Primary Role

Provide formal research structures and theoretical development.

---

## Category B

### Research Presentations

Examples

- Conference Presentations
- Slide Decks
- Demonstration Materials
- Executive Summaries

Primary Role

Provide condensed representations of research architecture.

---

## Category C

### Research Dialogues

Examples

- Interview Transcripts
- Scientific YouTube Transcripts
- Human–AI Dialogue Records
- Expert Conversations

Primary Role

Provide observable reasoning processes and knowledge emergence.

---

## Category D

### Research Repositories

Examples

- GitHub Repositories
- Documentation
- Methodology Collections
- Architecture Documents

Primary Role

Provide operational and organizational structures.

---

## Category E

### Operational Resources

Examples

- Observation Records
- Campaign Reports
- Design Memoranda
- Operational Protocols

Primary Role

Provide evidence of practical methodological application.

---

# Multiple Classification

A research resource may belong to more than one category.

For example,

A GitHub repository containing presentation materials may simultaneously function as:

- Research Repository
- Research Presentation

Classification therefore reflects observational purpose rather than file format.

---


# Future Expansion

Additional resource categories may be introduced as the Research Program expands.

Future classifications should remain compatible with the existing architectural structure while accommodating new forms of research communication.
