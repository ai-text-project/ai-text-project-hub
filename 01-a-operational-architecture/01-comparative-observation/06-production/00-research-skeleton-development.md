# Research Skeleton Development

**Architecture Version:** v0.1 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the process of Research Skeleton Development within the Comparative Observation Methodology.

The objective is to transform comparative insights into an organized structural foundation for future research assets.

Rather than producing completed research outputs, Research Skeleton Development establishes the logical structure that will guide subsequent blueprint design, research note development, and paper production.

It therefore represents the first stage of the Production layer.

---

# Development Objective

The objective of Research Skeleton Development is to organize comparative insights into a coherent structural framework that can support multiple forms of research communication.

Rather than creating complete manuscripts, this process identifies the essential organizational components that will later be expanded through successive production stages.

The resulting skeleton provides structural continuity throughout the entire production process.

---

# Input

Research Skeleton Development uses:

- Comparative Insights
- Correspondence Analysis
- Structural Interpretation
- Comparative Framework

All structural elements incorporated into the skeleton should remain traceable to the interpretation process.

No new observations should be introduced during this stage.

---

```
Comparative Insights

Structural Organization

        ▼

        │

        ▼

Blueprint Preparation
```

This process transforms interpreted knowledge into a reusable structural foundation.

---

# Structural Components

A Research Skeleton may include:

- research objective;
- central research question;
- organizational structure;
- major analytical sections;
- logical relationships between sections;
- anticipated research flow.

The level of detail should remain structural rather than manuscript-oriented.

---

# Methodological Principles

Research Skeleton Development follows several methodological principles.

- Preserve consistency with Comparative Insights.
- Maintain traceability to interpreted observations.
- Organize structure before expanding content.
- Avoid introducing unsupported theoretical claims.
- Design reusable research structures rather than domain-specific documents.

These principles ensure that subsequent production remains methodologically consistent.

---

# Relationship with Blueprint Development

Research Skeleton Development establishes the structural foundation of future research assets.

Blueprint Development subsequently expands this structural framework into a detailed production design.

```
Research Skeleton

        │

        ▼

Blueprint
```

The Blueprint elaborates the organizational logic established by the Research Skeleton without altering its structural foundation.

These developments should preserve the architecture-driven philosophy established within the Comparative Observation Methodology.
