# Blueprint Development

**Architecture Version:** v0.1 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the process of Blueprint Development within the Comparative Observation Methodology.

The objective of Blueprint Development is to transform the structural framework established by the Research Skeleton into a comprehensive production design that can guide the systematic development of research assets.

Whereas the Research Skeleton identifies the essential organizational structure of a future research product, the Blueprint specifies how that structure will be developed into coherent research communication.

Blueprint Development therefore bridges structural organization and practical research production.

# Development Objective

The objective of Blueprint Development is to provide an operational design for future research assets.

Rather than producing research content directly, Blueprint Development defines the logical organization, developmental sequence, and relationships among future components.

It serves as a production plan that guides subsequent writing activities while maintaining consistency with the Comparative Observation Methodology.

---

# Input

Blueprint Development uses:

- Research Skeleton
- Comparative Insights
- Correspondence Analysis
- Comparative Framework

All blueprint components should remain traceable to the structural organization established during previous methodological stages.

No additional observations or interpretations should be introduced during Blueprint Development.

---

---

# Blueprint Components

A Blueprint may define:

- research objectives;
- overall document organization;
- section hierarchy;
- logical progression of arguments;
- relationships between analytical components;
- expected development sequence;
- integration of comparative insights.

Each component should contribute to a coherent production design without becoming a complete manuscript.

---

# Methodological Principles

Blueprint Development follows several methodological principles.

- Preserve structural continuity established by the Research Skeleton.
- Maintain traceability to Comparative Insights.
- Separate structural planning from manuscript writing.
- Organize research before producing detailed content.
- Preserve consistency throughout the Production layer.

These principles ensure that production remains architecture-driven rather than document-driven.

---

# Relationship with Research Note Development

Blueprint Development establishes the production design for future research assets.

Research Note Development subsequently expands each blueprint component into detailed research descriptions while preserving the organizational structure defined by the Blueprint.

```
Blueprint

        │

        ▼

Research Notes
```

Research Notes elaborate the planned structure without altering the underlying production architecture.
