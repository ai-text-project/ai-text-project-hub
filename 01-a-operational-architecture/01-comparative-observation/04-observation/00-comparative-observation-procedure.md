# Comparative Observation Procedure

**Architecture Version:** v0.1 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the standard operational procedure for conducting comparative observation within the Comparative Observation Methodology.

The procedure transforms research resources into structured comparative understanding through a repeatable sequence of observation, interpretation, and research production.

The objective is to ensure methodological consistency while allowing flexibility across different comparative studies.

# Operational Workflow

The standard comparative observation process consists of six operational steps.

```
Corpus

      │

      ▼

      ▼

      │

      ▼

      │

      ▼

      │

      ▼

```

Each step prepares the foundation for the subsequent analytical process.

---

# Step 1

## Resource Selection

Objective

Identify research resources that are appropriate for the current comparative study.

Input

Managed Corpus

Output

Observation Set

---

# Step 2

## Individual Observation

Objective

Observe each selected research resource independently.

Focus

- major concepts
- reasoning process
- structural organization
- developmental characteristics

No comparison is performed during this stage.

---

# Step 3

## Comparative Observation

Objective

Compare observations across multiple research resources.

Focus

- recurring structures
- organizational similarities
- complementary perspectives
- structural differences

This stage produces comparative observations rather than interpretations.

---

# Step 4

## Structural Observation

Objective

Identify structural correspondence across observed resources.

Typical observations include:

- functional correspondence
- organizational patterns
- developmental relationships
- coordination structures

Structural observation transforms comparative results into higher-level organizational understanding.

---

# Step 5

## Observation Record

Objective

Document the observed structural relationships.

Observation records should preserve:

- compared resources
- comparison units
- observed correspondence
- preliminary comments

Interpretation is intentionally postponed.

---

# Relationship with Interpretation

The Observation layer concludes with documented structural observations.

Interpretation begins only after observational evidence has been recorded.

Accordingly,

```
Observation

        │

        ▼

Interpretation
```

maintains a clear separation between observation and explanation.

---

# Operational Principles

The procedure follows several operational principles.

- Observe before interpreting.
- Compare structures rather than conclusions.
- Preserve traceability.
- Record observations before synthesizing insights.
- Maintain methodological neutrality.

---

# Future Development

Future versions may incorporate:

- collaborative comparative observation;
- automated structural support tools;
- visualization of comparative observations; and
- observation workflow optimization.

Such developments should preserve the fundamental observational sequence defined in this procedure.
