# Structural Observation Guidelines

**Architecture Version:** v0.1 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the guiding principles for conducting Structural Observation within the Comparative Observation Methodology.

The objective is to support consistent identification of structural relationships while maintaining observational neutrality and methodological transparency.

These guidelines assist observers in recognizing structural correspondence without prematurely introducing interpretation.

# Observation Objective

The objective of Structural Observation is to identify organizational structures that appear across multiple research resources.

Observation focuses on how structures are organized rather than whether individual ideas are correct.

Accordingly, Structural Observation seeks recurring organizational patterns rather than conceptual agreement.

---

# Observation Perspective

Structural Observation emphasizes:

- organization;
- relationships;
- coordination;
- developmental processes; and
- functional roles.

The primary question is:

> "How is this research resource structurally organized?"

rather than

> "Is this conclusion correct?"

---

# Structural Observation Focus

Typical observational targets include:

- reasoning structures;
- knowledge organization;
- workflow organization;
- interaction patterns;
- developmental sequences;
- coordination mechanisms;
- transformation processes.

These categories are illustrative rather than exhaustive.

---

# Structural Correspondence

Structural correspondence may be observed when independently developed resources exhibit similar organizational relationships.

Possible examples include:

- comparable reasoning sequences;
- analogous organizational roles;
- recurring developmental structures;
- equivalent coordination patterns.

Correspondence does not imply identical terminology or identical implementation.

---

# Structural Difference

Structural differences should also be documented.

Differences may include:

- alternative organizational strategies;
- different developmental pathways;
- distinct coordination mechanisms;
- unique structural characteristics.

Differences contribute to comparative understanding and should not be regarded as methodological inconsistencies.

---

# Observational Neutrality

Observers should distinguish clearly between:

- observation;
- description; and
- interpretation.

At this stage, observations should remain descriptive.

Interpretative explanation is intentionally deferred to the Interpretation layer.

---

# Traceability

Every structural observation should remain traceable to:

- observed resources;
- comparison units;
- observation records; and
- comparative framework.

Traceability supports reproducibility and methodological consistency.

---

# Operational Principles

Structural Observation follows several operational principles.

- Observe before interpreting.
- Describe before explaining.
- Compare organization rather than conclusions.
- Preserve multiple structural possibilities.
- Maintain methodological transparency.

---

# Relationship with Interpretation

Structural Observation concludes once organizational patterns have been documented.

Interpretation subsequently examines the significance of these observations.

```
Structural Observation

        │

        ▼

Structural Interpretation
```

This separation preserves the distinction between evidence and explanation.

---

# Future Development

Future versions may incorporate:

- refined structural observation criteria;
- collaborative observation protocols;
- visualization techniques;
- computational support for structural comparison.

These developments should remain consistent with the observational principles defined here.
