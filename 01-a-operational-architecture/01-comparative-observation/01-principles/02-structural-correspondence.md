# Structural Correspondence

**Architecture Version:** v0.1 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the concept of Structural Correspondence within the Comparative Observation Methodology.

Rather than identifying identical concepts across research resources, the methodology seeks to identify recurring structural organizations that perform comparable functional roles.

Structural Correspondence therefore serves as the primary analytical principle for comparative observation.

# Definition

Structural Correspondence refers to the identification of functionally comparable structures across independent research resources.

The objective is not to demonstrate conceptual identity.

Instead, the methodology examines whether different resources organize knowledge in structurally similar ways.

---

# Fundamental Assumption

Different research resources may use different terminology, different theoretical backgrounds, and different explanatory styles.

Despite these differences, they may still exhibit comparable structural organizations.

Comparative observation therefore focuses on structural organization rather than conceptual equivalence.

---

# Characteristics of Structural Correspondence

Structural Correspondence is evaluated according to several complementary perspectives.

## Functional Role

Do two structures perform comparable functions?

Examples

- hypothesis generation
- knowledge organization
- collaborative reasoning

---

## Organizational Pattern

Are the internal organizational relationships similar?

Examples

- hierarchical organization
- iterative development
- cyclic refinement
- progressive integration

---

## Developmental Process

Do the structures evolve through comparable developmental pathways?

Examples

- incremental refinement
- repeated observation
- continuous restructuring

---

## System Position

Do the observed structures occupy similar positions within their respective systems?

Examples

- entry layer
- coordination layer
- interpretation layer
- production layer

# What Structural Correspondence Is Not

Structural Correspondence does not require:

- identical terminology
- identical theoretical assumptions
- identical conclusions
- causal relationships

Instead, it focuses on comparable structural organization.

---

# Role within Comparative Observation

Structural Correspondence enables the methodology to compare heterogeneous research resources within a common analytical framework.

Without Structural Correspondence, comparative observation would remain limited to superficial content comparison.

With Structural Correspondence, deeper organizational similarities become observable.

---

# Relationship with Structural Interpretation

Structural Correspondence identifies recurring organizational patterns.

Structural Interpretation explains the significance of those patterns.

Therefore,

```
Structural Correspondence

        │

        ▼

Structural Interpretation
```

represents the transition from observation to understanding.
