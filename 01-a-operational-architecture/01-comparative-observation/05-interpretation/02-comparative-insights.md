# Comparative Insights

**Architecture Version:** v0.1 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document defines how structural interpretations and correspondence analyses are synthesized into comparative insights.

Unlike individual observations or correspondence analyses, comparative insights represent higher-level understandings that emerge from multiple structural comparisons.

These insights provide the conceptual bridge between interpretation and the subsequent production of research assets.

---

# Position within the Methodology

Comparative Observation Methodology consists of multiple architectural layers.

Within the Interpretation layer, the workflow is organized as follows:

Structural Interpretation

↓

Correspondence Analysis

↓

Comparative Insights

Comparative Insights therefore represent the final outcome of the Interpretation phase before the methodology proceeds to Production.

---

# Core Definition

A Comparative Insight is a generalized understanding derived from multiple structural interpretations and correspondence analyses.

Rather than describing individual observations, comparative insights identify recurring structural relationships that are sufficiently stable to support further research development.

They summarize the implications of comparative observation without introducing new observational evidence.

---

# Relationship to Previous Components

Comparative Insights depend on the outputs of the preceding Interpretation documents.

Structural Interpretation establishes the meaning of individual structural observations.

Correspondence Analysis examines structural relationships between comparison targets.

Comparative Insights integrate these analyses into coherent methodological knowledge.

Each stage builds upon the previous one without redefining its responsibilities.

---

# Relationship to Production

Comparative Insights serve as the primary input to the Production layer.

The Production layer does not reinterpret observations.

Instead, it transforms comparative insights into structured research assets, including research skeletons, blueprints, research notes, and papers.

The transition from Interpretation to Production therefore represents a shift from methodological understanding to research development.

---

# Methodological Principles

Comparative Insights should remain:

- architecture-driven
- structurally grounded
- independent of individual research domains
- derived from multiple comparative observations
- reusable across future comparative studies

The objective is to preserve methodological consistency rather than produce domain-specific conclusions.

---

# Future Development

Future versions of the methodology may introduce additional techniques for organizing or visualizing comparative insights.

However, the fundamental role of Comparative Insights as the final synthesis within the Interpretation layer should remain unchanged.

Future extensions should enhance operational usability while preserving the overall architectural structure of the methodology.
