# Structural Interpretation

**Architecture Version:** v0.1 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the process of Structural Interpretation within the Comparative Observation Methodology.

Structural Interpretation examines the meaning and significance of structural observations recorded during the Observation layer.

Its objective is to transform documented structural correspondence into coherent comparative understanding while preserving traceability to the original observations.

---

# Interpretation Objective

The objective of Structural Interpretation is to understand the organizational significance of structural observations.

Interpretation seeks to explain how observed structural correspondence contributes to comparative understanding.

The focus is placed on organizational meaning rather than evaluative judgment.

---

# Input

Structural Interpretation begins with documented observational evidence.

Typical inputs include:

- Comparative Records
- Structural Observation Records
- Comparison Units
- Structural Correspondence

Interpretation should remain grounded in documented observations.

---

# Interpretation Process

The standard interpretation process consists of four stages.

```
Structural Observation

        │

        ▼

Observed Correspondence

        │

        ▼

Structural Interpretation

        │

        ▼

Comparative Understanding
```

Each stage builds upon the preceding observational evidence.

---

# Interpretation Perspective

Structural Interpretation examines questions such as:

- What organizational relationships are consistently observed?
- What functional roles appear across multiple resources?
- What developmental structures emerge repeatedly?
- How do structural differences contribute to comparative understanding?

Interpretation emphasizes organizational meaning rather than theoretical preference.

---

# Interpretation Principles

Structural Interpretation follows several methodological principles.

- Base interpretation on documented observations.
- Preserve traceability to observational evidence.
- Distinguish observation from explanation.
- Consider multiple possible interpretations.
- Maintain methodological transparency.

Interpretation should remain open to future refinement.

---

# Relationship with Observation

Structural Interpretation depends upon completed observation.

```
Observation

        │

        ▼

Structural Interpretation
```

Interpretation should never replace or modify the original observational record.

Instead, it builds upon the observational evidence already documented.

---

# Relationship with Comparative Insights

Structural Interpretation produces structured understanding.

Comparative Insights synthesize multiple interpretations into broader research knowledge.

Accordingly,

```
Structural Interpretation

        │

        ▼

Comparative Insights
```

maintains a progressive developmental sequence.

---

# Future Development

Future versions may incorporate:

- multi-perspective interpretation;

- interpretation visualization;
- computational support for interpretation tracking.

These developments should preserve the separation between observation and interpretation.
