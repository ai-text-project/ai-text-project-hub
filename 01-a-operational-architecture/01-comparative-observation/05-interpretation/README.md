# Interpretation

**Comparative Observation Methodology v0.1 (Provisional)**

---

# Overview

This folder defines the Interpretation layer of the Comparative Observation Methodology.

The Interpretation layer transforms structured observational evidence into reusable methodological knowledge.

Rather than introducing new observations, this layer analyzes structural relationships identified during comparative observation and develops higher-level methodological understanding.

The Interpretation layer therefore serves as the conceptual bridge between systematic observation and research production.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this folder is to transform comparative observations into structural insights.

This layer organizes observed structural correspondences, identifies meaningful patterns, and develops comparative insights that can support future research production.

Interpretation does not generate new observational evidence.

Instead, it derives methodological understanding from evidence collected during the Observation stage.

# Relationship with Adjacent Layers

The Interpretation layer analyzes that evidence and develops structural understanding.

The Production layer subsequently transforms these methodological insights into research assets.
