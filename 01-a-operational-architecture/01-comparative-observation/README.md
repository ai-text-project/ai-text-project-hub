# Comparative Observation Methodology

**Architecture Version:** v0.1 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

The Comparative Observation Methodology defines a systematic approach for identifying recurring structural organizations across heterogeneous research resources.

Rather than comparing theories, conclusions, or terminology directly, the methodology focuses on structural correspondence that emerges across independent observations.

The objective is to transform repeated comparative observations into reusable research methodology.

---

# Why This Methodology Emerged

The Comparative Observation Methodology was not designed as an abstract theoretical framework from the beginning.

Instead, it gradually emerged through repeated comparative observations conducted across multiple research domains.

These observations included:

- ACIM
- Psychology
- Cognitive Science
- Artificial Intelligence Research
- Scientific YouTube Transcripts
- Human–AI Collaboration Studies

Although these resources differed substantially in terminology, theoretical assumptions, and disciplinary background, they repeatedly exhibited comparable structural organizations.

Initially, the research focused on comparing individual research resources.

However, continuous observation gradually shifted the research emphasis toward comparing the underlying structures shared by those resources.

This repeated experience eventually motivated the explicit formulation of the Comparative Observation Methodology.

---

# Fundamental Perspective

The methodology assumes that different research resources may describe similar structural organizations using different conceptual languages.

Accordingly, comparative observation investigates:

- structural organization
- functional correspondence
- developmental processes
- organizational relationships

rather than conceptual identity.

The methodology therefore prioritizes structural understanding over terminology comparison.

Its primary role is to transform repeated comparative observations into reusable methodological knowledge.

# Development Philosophy

The Comparative Observation Methodology is regarded as an evolving research asset.

Its architecture is expected to mature through repeated comparative observation rather than through a single design process.

Accordingly, both methodological refinement and formation history are considered integral components of the Research Program.

Future comparative observations are expected to extend the methodology while preserving its architectural continuity.

# Role of Each Folder

The Comparative Structural Analysis methodology is organized into the following functional components.

| Folder | Role |
|--------|------|
| **00-architecture** | Overall design philosophy and architectural structure of the methodology. |
| **01-principles** | Fundamental principles that guide comparative structural observation. |
| **02-framework** | Design of the comparative analytical framework and structural comparison axes. |
| **03-corpus** | Management of research resources that serve as the observation corpus. |
| **04-observation** | Procedures for selecting materials from the corpus and conducting comparative structural observations. |
| **05-interpretation** | Processes for interpreting structural correspondences and transforming observations into research insights. |
| **06-production** | Development of research outputs, including Skeletons, Blueprints, Research Notes, and Papers. |
