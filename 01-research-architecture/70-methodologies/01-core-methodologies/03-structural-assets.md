# Structural Assets

**Methodology Version:** v1.0 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the reusable structural assets repeatedly generated through the Representation Pipeline.

The objective is not to describe assets belonging to any specific Structural Representation repository.

Instead, this document externalizes the common structural assets consistently observed across independently completed Structural Representation projects.

Accordingly, the Structural Assets constitute one of the primary methodological components of Structural Representation Methodology.

---

# Observation Basis

The present asset architecture has been extracted from completed Structural Representation repositories.

Representative observations include:

- ACIM Structural Representation Repository
- World Model Structural Representation
- Comparative Observation Repository

Only assets repeatedly observed across completed repositories are included.

# Primary Structural Assets

The completed repositories repeatedly generate the following structural assets.

## Structural Skeleton

Defines the primary organizational framework of the represented resource.

The Structural Skeleton provides the highest-level structural organization.

---

## Layer Map

Organizes the represented resource into reusable organizational layers.

Layer Maps preserve hierarchical continuity.

---

## Module Map

Identifies the principal organizational modules within the representation.

Modules function as reusable structural components.

---

## Dependency Map

Documents organizational dependencies among structural modules.

Dependencies preserve structural consistency throughout the representation.

---

## Architecture Matrix

Externalizes structural relationships in matrix form.

The Architecture Matrix provides an integrated view of organizational interactions.

---

## Concept Network

Represents structural connectivity among organizational units.

The network emphasizes organizational relationships rather than conceptual definitions.

---

## Structural Topology

Represents the global organizational structure resulting from the interaction of all structural assets.

Topology externalizes the overall organizational architecture.

---

## Canonical Structural Representation

Integrates all preceding structural assets into a stable organizational coordinate system.

This representation functions as the primary structural interface for future structural observation and comparative research.

---

## Structural Summary

Provides a concise organizational overview while preserving structural continuity.

The summary serves as the final representation of the completed structural organization.

# Methodological Characteristics

The observed Structural Assets consistently exhibit several methodological characteristics.

- Assets externalize organizational structure rather than conceptual content.
- Assets preserve structural continuity.
- Assets accumulate progressively throughout the Representation Pipeline.
- Canonical Structural Representation integrates all preceding assets.
- Repository organization preserves the relationships among generated assets.

These characteristics repeatedly appear across independently completed repositories.

---

# Relationship to Validation

The completed Structural Assets become the primary objects of Structural Validation.

Validation therefore evaluates:

- organizational consistency;
- structural continuity;
- dependency preservation;
- canonical integrity.

Validation methodology is documented separately.

---

# Summary

Structural Assets represent the reusable organizational outputs of the Representation Pipeline.

Rather than functioning as isolated deliverables, the assets collectively externalize the organizational architecture of the represented research resource.

The resulting asset architecture constitutes one of the central methodological components of Structural Representation Methodology.
