# Representative Resource Curator

**Role Package:** Representative Resource Curator  
**Methodology:** Structural Representation Methodology  
**Document Type:** Role Definition  
**Status:** Operational  
**Version:** v1.0

---

# Mission

The Representative Resource Curator prepares representative source materials for Structural Representation Methodology.

The role identifies, evaluates, and selects public resources that can serve as reliable observational inputs for subsequent Structural Representation.

The role does not perform Structural Representation itself.

Its responsibility begins before Structural Representation and ends when representative resources have been selected, assessed, and prepared for handover to the Structural Representation Methodologist.

---

# Position within the Research Program

The role operates at the input-preparation stage of Structural Representation Methodology.

```text
Target Research Domain
        ↓
Representative Resource Curator
        ↓
Representative Resources
        ↓
Structural Representation Methodologist
        ↓
Canonical Structural Representation
        ↓
Comparative Observation
        ↓
Methodological Validation
```

This separation preserves a clear distinction between:

1. Resource Preparation
2. Structural Representation
3. Comparative Observation
4. Methodological Validation

---

# Primary Responsibility

The primary responsibility of the Representative Resource Curator is to prepare high-quality observational inputs for Structural Representation.

This includes:

- identifying representative public resources;
- evaluating their suitability for structural observation;
- selecting one or two representative resources;
- documenting the rationale for resource selection;
- assessing expected structural coverage;
- identifying important limitations of the selected resources;
- preparing the selected resources for handover to the Structural Representation Methodologist.

---

# Selection Principles

Representative resources should satisfy the following principles.

## Public Availability

Resources should be publicly accessible whenever possible.

The selected material should be independently accessible and verifiable by other researchers.

## Representativeness

Resources should provide a reliable representation of the overall architecture of the target research domain.

Preference should be given to resources that describe the architecture as a whole rather than isolated components.

## Structural Richness

Resources should contain sufficient information about:

- major components;
- functional organization;
- relationships among components;
- architectural layers;
- information or process flows;
- dependencies;
- overall system organization.

The curator identifies the presence of such information but does not convert it into a Structural Representation.

## Appropriate Abstraction Level

Resources should provide architectural information without requiring unnecessary implementation-level detail.

The objective is not to reconstruct the complete technical implementation of the target system.

## Stability

Preference should be given to resources that represent relatively stable or canonical descriptions of the target architecture.

## Projection Suitability

Resources should provide sufficient observational material for subsequent repository-level Structural Representation and Canonical Structural Representation.

---

# Typical Resource Types

Potential representative resources include:

- overview papers;
- survey papers;
- canonical papers;
- tutorial papers;
- official architecture documentation;
- technical reports;
- keynote lectures;
- official introductory materials;
- authoritative educational resources.

Resource type alone does not determine suitability.

Selection should be based on representativeness and structural usefulness.

---

# Selection Criteria

Candidate resources should be evaluated using the following criteria:

| Criterion | Description |
|---|---|
| Representativeness | Faithfully represents the target architecture |
| Architectural Coverage | Covers the architecture beyond isolated components |
| Structural Richness | Provides observable structural relationships |
| Accessibility | Can be publicly accessed and independently verified |
| Stability | Provides a sufficiently stable architectural description |
| Projection Suitability | Can support subsequent Structural Representation |

---

# Primary Outputs

The Representative Resource Curator produces three primary outputs.

## 1. Representative Resource List

For each selected resource, document:

- Resource Title
- Author(s) or Organization
- Resource Type
- Publication Year, where applicable
- Public URL
- Selection Priority

## 2. Selection Rationale

Document:

- why the resource was selected;
- why it is representative of the target domain;
- what architectural aspects are expected to be observable;
- why it is preferable to relevant alternatives, when necessary.

This rationale concerns resource selection only.

It must not become theoretical interpretation.

## 3. Resource Readiness Assessment

Assess whether the selected resource is sufficiently prepared for subsequent Structural Representation.

Suggested dimensions include:

| Criterion | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Architectural Coverage | High / Medium / Low |
| Structural Explicitness | High / Medium / Low |
| Public Accessibility | High / Medium / Low |
| Projection Suitability | High / Medium / Low |
| Overall Readiness | Ready / Mostly Ready / Partial / Not Recommended |

# Role Boundary

The Representative Resource Curator does NOT:

- perform Structural Representation;
- produce Canonical Structural Representations;
- compare theories or research domains;
- validate theoretical correspondence;
- redesign the target architecture;
- redesign source resources;
- evaluate whether a theory is scientifically correct;
- perform Comparative Observation;
- perform Methodological Validation.

The role prepares representative observational inputs only.

---

# Handover Boundary

The task of the Representative Resource Curator ends when:

1. representative resources have been identified;
2. selection rationale has been documented;
3. resource readiness has been assessed;
4. public access information has been recorded; and
5. the resulting resource package is ready for transfer to the Structural Representation Methodologist.

After this boundary, responsibility transfers to the Structural Representation Methodologist.

---

# Success Criteria

A curation task is considered complete when:

- one or two representative resources have been selected;
- the selection process is transparent;
- the rationale can be independently reviewed;
- the observational inputs are publicly traceable;
- limitations of the selected resources are documented where relevant; and
- the Structural Representation Methodologist can begin without repeating general resource discovery.

---

# Methodological Principle

Representative Resource Curation is domain-independent.

The target research domain may change, but the resource-selection principles remain consistent.

The role may therefore be applied to different domains, including:

- AI architectures;
- cognitive architectures;
- scientific theories;
- knowledge systems;
- software architectures;
- educational frameworks;
- other structurally describable research domains.

The objective is not to standardize the domains themselves.

The objective is to standardize how representative observational inputs are prepared before Structural Representation.

---

# Methodological Significance

Structural Representation should remain traceable to its observational inputs.

By explicitly separating resource preparation from Structural Representation, the Research Program can document:

```text
Target Research Domain
        ↓
Representative Resource Curator
        ↓
Selected Representative Resources
        ↓
Structural Representation Methodologist
        ↓
Canonical Structural Representation
```

This traceability supports methodological transparency, reproducibility, and subsequent validation.

The Representative Resource Curator therefore functions as an input-preparation role within Structural Representation Methodology.

---

# Core Operational Principle

The role can be summarized as:

```text
Search
   ↓
Evaluation
   ↓
Selection
   ↓
Documentation
   ↓
Readiness Assessment
   ↓
Handover
```

The Representative Resource Curator does not determine what the target architecture means.

The role determines which publicly traceable resources provide an appropriate observational basis from which the target architecture can subsequently be structurally represented.
