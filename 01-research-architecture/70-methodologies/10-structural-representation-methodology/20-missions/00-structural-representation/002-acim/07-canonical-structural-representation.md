This document presents the Canonical Structural Representation of ACIM.

Unlike the Structural Topology, which integrates the complete organizational architecture, the Canonical Structural Representation projects the completed topology into a reusable structural interface.

The objective is not to introduce additional structural observations.

Instead, this document externalizes the completed organizational architecture into a stable representation suitable for future Comparative Observation.

The Canonical Structural Representation preserves the completed Structural Topology without modification.

The resulting Representation remains independent of:

- theological interpretation;

- comparative analysis;
- scientific evaluation.
