# Purpose

This document summarizes the structural differences, unresolved observations, and remaining validation uncertainties identified throughout the Structural Representation validation process.

Unlike the Agreement Map, which integrates confirmed structural consistencies, the Difference Map records structural elements that were not fully confirmed, remain unresolved, or require future investigation.

The objective is not to identify errors.

Instead, this document preserves methodological transparency by explicitly documenting the current limits of structural validation.

# Difference Principle

Difference is evaluated at the level of structural organization rather than lexical variation.

Accordingly,

a structural difference represents an unresolved organizational observation rather than evidence of inconsistency.

The absence of confirmed agreement should not automatically be interpreted as disagreement.

# Completion Criterion

The Difference Map is considered complete when all remaining structural uncertainties have been explicitly documented and no unresolved inconsistency requiring immediate repository modification remains.

The resulting Difference Map should function as the methodological record of the current limits of structural validation.

# Design Freeze

The Difference Map records the validation status obtained during the present repository evaluation.

Future revisions should occur only when additional reference resources or comparative studies reveal new structural evidence.
