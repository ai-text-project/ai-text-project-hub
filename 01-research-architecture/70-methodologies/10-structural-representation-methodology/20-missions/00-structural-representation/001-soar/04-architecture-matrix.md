# SOAR Architecture Matrix

**Representation Version:** v0.1 (Observation Draft)

# Purpose

This document integrates the structural observations established in the preceding representation stages into a unified architectural matrix.

Its objective is not to introduce additional structural elements.

Instead, it organizes the previously identified structural layers, modules, and dependencies into a coherent organizational framework.

Accordingly, the Architecture Matrix serves as the first integrated representation of the SOAR architecture.

---

# Observation Basis

The present Architecture Matrix is derived from the following completed structural assets:

- Structural Skeleton
- Layer Map
- Module Map
- Dependency Map

No additional structural components are introduced.

The matrix reorganizes existing observations into an integrated architectural perspective.

# Observation Principle

The Architecture Matrix preserves the organizational continuity established throughout the preceding representation stages.

It integrates:

- hierarchical organization;
- structural modules;
- dependency relationships;

while intentionally excluding implementation details.

The objective is to reveal the overall organizational architecture rather than individual structural elements.
