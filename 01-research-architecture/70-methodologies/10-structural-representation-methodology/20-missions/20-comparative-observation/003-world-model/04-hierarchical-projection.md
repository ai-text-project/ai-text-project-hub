**Observation Version:** v1.0 (Provisional)

# Purpose

This document projects the organizational architectures of the World Model and the AI Textbook onto a common hierarchical observation space.

The objective is not to establish theoretical equivalence.

Instead, the purpose is to investigate whether independently developed representations exhibit comparable hierarchical organization.

The projection therefore visualizes observed organizational relationships while preserving the conceptual independence of both research resources.

---

# Projection Principle

The projection follows the principle:

**Project organizational relationships rather than conceptual labels.**

Each hierarchy is interpreted according to its organizational function within its own representation.

Observed relationships are projected without assuming one-to-one correspondence.

# Projection Characteristics

The following characteristics have been observed.

## 1. Progressive Layer Organization

Both representations organize development through successive hierarchical layers.

---

## 2. Layer Dependency

Higher layers depend upon organizational structures established at lower layers.

Hierarchical progression therefore appears cumulative.

---

## 3. Functional Expansion

Each successive layer introduces additional organizational capability.

The hierarchy expands organizational complexity rather than replacing earlier structures.

---

## 4. Organizational Continuity

Both representations maintain continuous organizational development across hierarchical transitions.

No observed layer appears structurally isolated.

---

# Interpretation Boundary

The present projection should not be interpreted as evidence that corresponding layers describe identical concepts.

The projection visualizes organizational positioning only.

Conceptual comparison remains outside the scope of the present document.
