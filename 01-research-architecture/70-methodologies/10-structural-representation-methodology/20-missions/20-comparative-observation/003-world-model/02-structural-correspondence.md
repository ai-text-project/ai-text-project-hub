# Purpose

This document investigates whether structural correspondence exists between the organizational architecture of the World Model and the developmental architecture extracted from the AI Textbook.

The objective is not to establish theoretical equivalence.

Instead, the purpose is to observe structural relationships while preserving the conceptual independence of both research resources.

---

# Comparative Principle

The comparison follows the principle:

**Compare Representations, not Resources.**

Both research resources have already been independently transformed into structural representations.

Structural correspondence is therefore investigated between their representations rather than their original documents.

---

# Observation Scope

This document investigates structural correspondence only.

The following are intentionally excluded.

- Terminology comparison
- Chapter comparison
- Scientific validation
- Theoretical integration
- Functional interpretation

These topics are addressed separately in later documents.

# Initial Structural Observation

Both representations exhibit continuous organizational progression rather than independent collections of concepts.

Neither representation appears to be organized primarily around isolated topics.

Instead, both exhibit successive structural organization in which earlier stages establish conditions for later stages.

This organizational characteristic constitutes the first observed structural correspondence.

---

# Observed Structural Correspondence

The following structural characteristics have been observed.

## 1. Sequential Organization

Both representations are organized as developmental sequences.

The sequence itself functions as an architectural principle.

---

## 2. Dependency-Based Development

Each organizational stage depends upon preceding stages.

Structural progression is cumulative rather than independent.

---

## 3. Progressive Organization

Later organizational units emerge from earlier organizational units.

The observed architecture therefore exhibits developmental continuity.

---

## 4. Organizational Rather Than Topical Structure

Neither representation primarily organizes knowledge according to topics.

Instead, organizational relationships define the overall architecture.
