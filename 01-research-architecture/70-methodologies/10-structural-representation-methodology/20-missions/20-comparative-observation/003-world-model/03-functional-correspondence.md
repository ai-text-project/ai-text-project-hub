# Functional Correspondence

**Observation Version:** v1.0 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This document investigates whether functional correspondence exists between the organizational components of the World Model and the developmental architecture extracted from the AI Textbook.

The objective is not to establish conceptual equivalence.

Instead, the purpose is to observe whether independently developed structural units perform comparable organizational functions within their respective architectures.

---

# Comparative Principle

The comparison follows the principle:

**Compare Functions within Representations, not Terminology across Resources.**

Each structural unit is interpreted according to the organizational role it performs inside its own representation.

Functional correspondence is investigated only after structural correspondence has been established.

---

# Observation Scope

This document investigates:

- Organizational functions
- Functional dependencies
- Developmental roles
- Functional progression

The following are intentionally excluded.

- Terminology comparison
- Chapter comparison
- Scientific validation
- Theoretical integration

---

# World Model Functional Organization

The World Model organizes intelligence through successive functional stages.

| Structural Unit | Observed Function |
|-----------------|------------------|
| World | Provides the external environment to be modeled |
| Representation | Constructs an internal organizational model |
| Search | Explores possible solution spaces |
| Prediction | Estimates future states based on the representation |
| Planning | Organizes future actions into executable strategies |
| Action | Executes externally observable behavior |

Each function contributes to a continuous organizational process.

---

# AI Textbook Functional Organization

The AI Textbook organizes understanding through successive developmental functions.

| Development Unit | Observed Function |
|------------------|------------------|
| Orientation | Establishes the research perspective |
| Foundational Model | Provides conceptual foundations |
| Theory | Defines organizational principles |
| Mechanism | Explains operational mechanisms |
| Observation | Establishes systematic observation methods |
| Transformation | Enables structural development and application |

Each developmental unit prepares the conditions required for subsequent functional development.

---

# Initial Functional Observation

Although the organizational structures differ,

both representations assign distinct functional roles to successive developmental units.

Neither architecture appears to consist of independent functional modules.

Instead,

each function contributes to the progression of the overall organizational process.

---

# Observed Functional Correspondence

The following functional characteristics have been observed.

## 1. Foundation Function

Both representations begin by establishing an organizational foundation that supports subsequent development.

---

## 2. Progressive Functional Development

Later functions depend upon the successful completion of earlier organizational functions.

Functional development therefore appears cumulative rather than independent.

---

## 3. Organizational Coordination

Individual functions operate as components of a larger organizational architecture.

The emphasis is placed upon coordination rather than isolated capability.

---

## 4. Functional Continuity

The observed functions collectively contribute to continuous organizational progression.

No function appears to operate independently of the surrounding developmental context.

---
