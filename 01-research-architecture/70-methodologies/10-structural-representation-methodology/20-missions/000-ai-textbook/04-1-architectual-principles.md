# Purpose

This document records the architectural principles underlying each structural layer of the AI Textbook.

Unlike the Layer Map, which defines the role of each layer, this document describes the design principle through which each layer is organized.

The objective is not to summarize chapter contents, but to reveal the architectural logic governing the developmental progression of the textbook.

---
