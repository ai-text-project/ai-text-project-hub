# Purpose

This document records the developmental organization of the AI Textbook.

Unlike the Chapter Map, which records the structural placement of individual chapters, the Development Map focuses on how architectural units evolve across the textbook.

The objective is to reveal the developmental sequence of the textbook's internal architecture.

---

# Development Principle

The AI Textbook is not organized as an independent collection of chapters.

Instead, it follows a developmental progression in which each structural layer prepares the conditions for the next layer.

Development therefore occurs through architectural transformation rather than simple chapter accumulation.
