**Repository:** ACIM Structural Representation Repository

**Version:** v0.1 (Provisional)

# Purpose

This document defines the primary organizational layers of ACIM.

Unlike the Structural Skeleton, which establishes the highest architectural framework, the Layer Map identifies the major organizational layers that collectively compose the structural architecture of ACIM.

The objective is not to describe individual concepts.

The objective is not to define structural modules.

Instead, the Layer Map establishes the stable organizational layers that will later contain Functional Modules, Dependency Structures, Concept Networks, and Structural Topology.

# Layer Organization

The current Structural Representation identifies five primary organizational layers.

These layers were extracted from the structural observations conducted throughout the repository construction process.

# Boundary

This document does not describe:

- individual theological meanings;
- conceptual definitions;
- dependency relationships;
- structural pathways;
- comparative observations.

Its sole responsibility is the externalization of the primary organizational layers of ACIM.

---

# Completion Criterion

The Layer Map is considered complete when the highest organizational layers of ACIM can be understood independently of individual concepts and before any structural modules are introduced.

The resulting Layer Map should function as the stable organizational framework upon which all subsequent structural assets are progressively constructed.

---

# Design Freeze

Once approved, the organizational layers defined in this document should remain stable.

Future structural assets are expected to refine the internal organization of each layer rather than redefine the layers themselves.

Accordingly, modifications to the Layer Map should occur only when fundamental architectural inconsistencies are identified.


# Layer Evolution Policy

The organizational layers defined in this document are intentionally independent of individual concepts.

As new structural observations become available, new Functional Modules may be added to existing layers.

However, the introduction of new modules should not automatically create additional organizational layers.

The architectural stability of the Layer Map takes precedence over conceptual expansion.
