# Purpose

This document defines the highest-level structural skeleton of ACIM.

The objective is not to describe theological concepts.

The objective is not to summarize the original text.

Instead, this document establishes the primary organizational framework upon which all subsequent Structural Representations will be constructed.

The Structural Skeleton serves as the architectural foundation for the Layer Map, Module Map, Dependency Map, Architecture Matrix, Structural Topology, and Canonical Structural Representation.

# Repository Role

The Structural Skeleton is intentionally minimal.

Its responsibility is to define the highest structural organization of ACIM.

It does not define structural modules.

It does not define dependency relationships.

It does not define concept networks.

These responsibilities belong to subsequent structural assets.

---

Each subsequent document progressively refines the same structural architecture without modifying the Structural Skeleton itself.

---

# Completion Criterion

The Structural Skeleton is considered complete when the highest organizational architecture of ACIM can be understood independently of individual concepts and without reference to the original resource.

The resulting Skeleton should function as the stable architectural foundation of the entire Structural Representation Repository.

# Design Freeze

The Structural Skeleton represents the highest organizational architecture of ACIM.

Once approved, this Skeleton should remain stable throughout the development of the repository.

Subsequent structural assets are expected to refine the architecture rather than redefine it.

Accordingly, modifications to the Structural Skeleton should occur only when fundamental organizational inconsistencies are identified.
