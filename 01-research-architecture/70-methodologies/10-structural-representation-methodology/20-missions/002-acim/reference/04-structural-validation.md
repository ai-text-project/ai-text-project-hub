# Purpose

This document validates the internal structural consistency of the ACIM Structural Representation Repository.

Unlike Terminology Validation, which evaluates structural consistency against an external reference resource, Structural Validation evaluates the internal coherence of the repository itself.

The objective is not to validate terminology.

The objective is not to compare theological interpretations.

Instead, this document verifies that every structural asset generated throughout the Structural Representation process remains logically consistent with the overall organizational architecture.

Accordingly, Structural Validation represents the Internal Validation process of the repository.

---

# Validation Scope

Structural Validation evaluates the repository as an integrated organizational architecture.

Validation is performed across structural dimensions rather than individual repository assets.

The objective is to determine whether the completed Structural Representation maintains architectural consistency throughout the entire repository.

---

# Structural Dimension Validation

Structural Validation is organized according to six primary structural dimensions.

Unlike Terminology Validation, which validates repository assets sequentially, Structural Validation validates organizational dimensions that extend across the complete repository.

The six structural dimensions are:

- Layer Validation
- Dependency Validation
- Architecture Validation
- Network Validation
- Topology Validation
- Representation Validation

Each dimension evaluates the repository from a different organizational perspective while preserving the completed Structural Representation.
