# Structural Characteristics

Several structural characteristics emerge from the observation.

- Representation functions as the organizational foundation.
- Search operates upon the internal representation rather than directly upon the external world.
- Prediction depends upon both representation and search.
- Planning organizes future possibilities into executable decisions.
- Action concludes the organizational process by interacting with the external environment.

These characteristics describe structural relationships rather than algorithmic implementation.
