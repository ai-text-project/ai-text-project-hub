# Purpose

This document defines the current validation status and validation boundary of the Recursive Reasoning Protocol Candidate developed through Mission 002.

Its purpose is not to perform a new independent validation study.

Its purpose is not to rediscover or redesign the Protocol Candidate.

Its purpose is not to strengthen the Protocol by adding new mechanisms.

Instead, this document asks:

> **What aspects of the Recursive Reasoning Protocol Candidate are currently supported by Mission 002 evidence, what forms of examination have already occurred, what remains unvalidated, and what claims can therefore be made responsibly?**
