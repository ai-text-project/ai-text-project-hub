# Purpose

Mission 002 develops an operational Protocol Candidate for Recursive Reasoning.

The Mission begins after the pattern-discovery work of Mission 001.

Mission 001 established evidence that recursive reasoning could be observed as a recurring structural phenomenon.

Mission 002 asks a different question:

> **Can the observed Recursive Reasoning Pattern be transformed into a responsibility-differentiated, operationally coherent, and evidence-supported Protocol Candidate without prematurely converting the observation into a universal theory or implementation framework?**

The result is:

> **Recursive Reasoning Protocol Candidate**

with the current status:

```text
Evidence-Supported
+
Internally Examined
+
Mission-Bounded
```

Independent Validation has not yet been established.

# Mission Responsibility

Mission 002 is responsible for:

- defining the entry boundary for Protocol formation;
- identifying the operational requirements of Recursive Reasoning;
- differentiating the principal recursive mechanisms;
- examining when recursive re-entry is required;
- determining what prior reasoning should be revisited;
- determining how deeply recursive examination should proceed;
- distinguishing preservation from revision;
- defining selective revision;
- defining the conditions for Restabilization;
- representing Human–AI Coupling across the reasoning process;
- integrating the mechanisms into a Canonical Protocol Candidate;
- defining the current Validation Boundary;
- calibrating the evidential claims that may responsibly be made.

Mission 002 is not responsible for:

- establishing a universal theory of reasoning;
- proving universal validity;
- establishing quantitative superiority;
- implementing a production reasoning engine;
- defining a universal Human–AI governance architecture;
- independently validating the Protocol Candidate;
- establishing cross-task generalization;
- establishing cross-instance reproducibility.

## 03 — Recursive Trigger and Re-entry

**Primary Responsibility:**

> Determine when recursive reasoning should re-enter prior reasoning.

03 distinguishes:

```text
New Information
        ≠
Meaningful Trigger
```

and:

```text
Meaningful Trigger
        ≠
Automatic Re-entry
```

Its primary operational questions are:

```text
Has something materially changed?

Does that change require
recursive re-entry?
```

---

## 04 — Recursive Target and Depth

**Primary Responsibility:**

> Determine what prior reasoning should be revisited and how far recursive examination should proceed.

04 differentiates:

```text
Target Selection
```

from:

```text
Depth Selection
```

Its governing principle is bounded recursive examination.

```text
Re-enter
the smallest sufficient
reasoning target
        +
Recurse
only as deeply as
materially required
```

## 05 — Preservation and Revision

**Primary Responsibility:**

> Determine what remains valid and what must change after recursive re-examination.

05 establishes:

```text
Re-examination
        ≠
Automatic Revision
```

and supports:

```text
Preserve
what remains materially valid

+

Revise
what no longer remains
materially coherent
```

Revision remains selective rather than automatically reconstructive.

# Core Operational Principle

The integrated Protocol Candidate is governed by the following principle:

> **Recursive reasoning should re-enter prior reasoning only when materially warranted, revisit only what is structurally implicated, revise only what requires revision, preserve what remains valid, propagate relevant consequences, and stop when the updated Reasoning State has regained sufficient material coherence for continuation.**

Human and AI may participate throughout this process without collapsing:

```text
Contribution

Influence

Authority

Responsibility

Structural Validity
```

into one another.

---

# Recursive Economy

A recurring property of the Protocol Candidate is boundedness.

```text
Trigger
        ↓
Do not re-enter unnecessarily.

Target
        ↓
Do not reopen unnecessarily.

Depth
        ↓
Do not recurse unnecessarily far.

Revision
        ↓
Do not reconstruct unnecessarily.

Propagation
        ↓
Do not propagate unnecessarily.

Restabilization
        ↓
Do not demand unnecessary certainty.
```

This can be summarized as:

> **Use the smallest recursive intervention sufficient to restore material coherence.**

---

# Preservation Principle

Recursive Reasoning is not represented as automatic destruction and reconstruction.

Instead:

```text
Re-entry
        ↓
Re-examine
        ↓
Discriminate
        ↓
Preserve
+
Revise
        ↓
Reintegrate
        ↓
Restabilize
```

The Protocol therefore preserves valid prior reasoning whenever possible.
