# Mission 004 — Operational Entry Conditions

## Entry Conditions for Operational Observation

**Research Area:** Recursive Reasoning  
**Mission:** 004 — Operational Observation  
**Document:** 02 — Operational Entry Conditions  
**Status:** Active — Entry Condition Definition  
**Predecessor:** 01-observation-boundary.md

# Purpose

This document defines the conditions under which a reasoning situation may enter Mission 004 as an Operational Observation Case.

Mission 004 does not require an operational case merely because the Protocol exists and operational evidence remains limited.

A case becomes eligible when a genuine reasoning situation creates a legitimate need for recursive reconsideration.

The purpose of the Entry Conditions is therefore not to identify a case in which the Protocol is likely to succeed.

The purpose is to determine whether a naturally occurring reasoning situation is structurally appropriate for operational observation.

---

# Core Entry Principle

Mission 004 follows the principle:

> **The Protocol does not create the operational case. The reasoning situation creates the reason for Protocol entry.**

Therefore:

```text
Need to test the Protocol
        ↓
Construct / Select convenient case
        ✗
```

is not a legitimate entry route.

The appropriate route is:

```text
Existing Reasoning State
        ↓
Operational Development
        ↓
Possible Need for Reconsideration
        ↓
Entry Conditions
        ↓
Eligible Operational Case?
```

---

# Four Core Entry Conditions

A reasoning situation is eligible for Mission 004 when four conditions are sufficiently present:

```text
1. Existing Reasoning State

2. Reconsideration Trigger

3. Material Relevance

4. Genuine Operational Context
```

These conditions define case eligibility.

They do not predict Protocol success.

---

# Condition 1 — Existing Reasoning State

A recursive reasoning situation requires something to which reasoning can return.

There should therefore be an identifiable Existing Reasoning State.

This may include, for example:

- a current judgment;
- a provisional conclusion;
- a structural model;
- a research design;
- a candidate architecture;
- an interpretation;
- a decision;
- an operational plan;
- or another sufficiently formed reasoning state.

The Existing Reasoning State does not need to be final or formally frozen.

It must, however, be sufficiently identifiable that recursive reconsideration has a meaningful target.

Thus:

```text
Initial reasoning from no prior state
        ≠
Recursive Reconsideration

Existing reasoning state
        +
Possible need to revisit it
        →
Recursive Entry Candidate
```

---

# Condition 2 — Reconsideration Trigger

A development must occur that creates a plausible reason to reconsider the Existing Reasoning State.

Possible triggers may include:

- a new observation;
- new evidence;
- contradiction;
- unexpected behavior;
- operational failure;
- ambiguity;
- counterexample;
- external challenge;
- changed condition;
- unresolved dependency;
- or another development capable of affecting the current reasoning.

The trigger does not need to prove that revision is necessary.

It needs only to create a legitimate question about whether reconsideration is required.

Therefore:

```text
Trigger
        ≠
Revision Requirement

Trigger
        →
Reconsideration Question
```

---

# Condition 3 — Material Relevance

The trigger should have a plausible relation to the substance or structure of the Existing Reasoning State.

Trivial wording changes, cosmetic preferences, or developments with no meaningful relation to the current reasoning do not by themselves establish operational entry.

The relevant question is:

> **Could this development materially affect the reasoning currently being preserved or used?**

Mission 004 does not introduce a new universal definition or threshold of Materiality.

Materiality already operates within the frozen Protocol and was identified during Mission 003 as an area requiring qualification.

Mission 004 therefore treats Materiality as an operational judgment to be observed rather than repaired in advance.

Accordingly:

```text
Materiality needed for Entry
        ✓

New universal Materiality rule
created before Observation
        ✗
```

Any ambiguity encountered when making a Materiality judgment may itself become operational evidence.

---

# Condition 4 — Genuine Operational Context

The reasoning situation should originate from genuine research, analytical, design, decision, or other substantive reasoning activity.

It should not be created primarily for the purpose of demonstrating or validating the Recursive Reasoning Protocol.

The governing distinction is:

```text
Reasoning activity exists
for its own substantive purpose
        ↓
Recursive reconsideration becomes relevant
        ✓
```

versus:

```text
Need an Operational Observation Case
        ↓
Create or select reasoning activity
primarily to exercise the Protocol
        ✗
```

A Genuine Operational Context does not require that the situation be external to the current research environment.

It requires that the substantive reasoning purpose exist independently of the desire to produce favorable Protocol evidence.

---

# Entry Eligibility

The four conditions operate together:

```text
Existing Reasoning State
        +
Reconsideration Trigger
        +
Material Relevance
        +
Genuine Operational Context
        ↓
Operational Case Eligible
```

The Entry Conditions should be interpreted as structural requirements rather than as a scoring system.

Mission 004 does not require arbitrary numerical thresholds.

The entry judgment should remain inspectable and evidence-bounded.

---

# Entry Does Not Predict Outcome

Eligibility for Mission 004 does not imply that:

- the Protocol will operate smoothly;
- revision will occur;
- preservation will occur;
- every Protocol mechanism will be exercised;
- the Protocol will resolve the reasoning problem;
- or the resulting evidence will support the Protocol.

Therefore:

```text
Eligible Operational Case
        ≠
Successful Protocol Case
```

Case eligibility concerns the legitimacy of observation.

Protocol behavior remains an empirical question within the operational case.

---

# Mechanism Coverage Is Not an Entry Requirement

An operational case does not need to exercise every Protocol mechanism.

A case may legitimately exercise only part of the Protocol.

For example:

```text
Trigger / Re-entry        EXERCISED
Target / Depth            EXERCISED
Preserve / Revise         EXERCISED
Restabilization           EXERCISED
Recursive Return          NOT EXERCISED
```

This remains a valid operational observation.

Mission 004 should not prefer cases merely because they appear likely to exercise more Protocol components.

Mechanisms not exercised by the case should later be recorded as:

> **NOT EXERCISED**

rather than treated as supported or refuted.

---

# Known-Finding Protection

Mission 003 identified known Revision Candidates concerning:

```text
Materiality

Preserve → Restabilization

Recursive-return Routing
```

These known findings do not themselves establish Mission 004 entry.

A case should not be selected because it appears likely to reproduce one of these findings.

Therefore:

```text
Known Revision Candidate
        ↓
Search for matching case
        ✗
```

However, if an otherwise eligible operational case naturally encounters one of these issues, the event may be preserved under the Observation Boundary.

---

# Retrospective Case Protection

A reasoning episode whose outcome is already known should not ordinarily be reconstructed and presented as if it were a live Operational Observation Case.

Historical cases may have research value.

They create a different evidence relation.

Mission 004 prioritizes prospective or sufficiently contemporaneous observation in which the operational behavior of the Protocol can be preserved without reconstructing the case around its known outcome.

Therefore:

```text
Natural Situation
        ↓
Entry Judgment
        ↓
Operational Observation
        ✓
```

is preferred over:

```text
Known Historical Outcome
        ↓
Retrospective Reconstruction
        ↓
Presented as Operational Observation
        ✗
```

Historical material may later be used under an explicitly different evidence classification if justified.

---

# Natural Does Not Mean Accidental

A Natural Operational Case does not need to occur unexpectedly.

The research environment may deliberately preserve awareness that recursive reconsideration could become relevant.

What should not be predetermined is the case outcome or the need to demonstrate Protocol success.

Therefore:

```text
Prepared to recognize
a legitimate operational opportunity
        ✓

Manufacture an opportunity
to produce Protocol evidence
        ✗
```

This distinction allows Mission 004 to remain operationally ready without turning case discovery into case construction.

---

# Human–AI Context

A reasoning situation occurring within Human–AI dialogue may satisfy the Entry Conditions.

Human–AI dialogue is neither automatically eligible nor automatically excluded.

The same four conditions apply:

```text
Existing Reasoning State
        +
Reconsideration Trigger
        +
Material Relevance
        +
Genuine Operational Context
```

If the case occurs within a Human–AI relationship historically connected to Protocol formation, that relationship should later be recorded as part of the evidence context.

Such a case may produce operational evidence without automatically producing independent evidence.

---

# Entry Rejection

A candidate situation should not enter Mission 004 when:

- no identifiable Existing Reasoning State exists;
- no plausible Reconsideration Trigger exists;
- the trigger has no material relation to the reasoning state;
- the situation was created primarily to demonstrate the Protocol;
- the case is selected primarily because a favorable result is expected;
- the case is selected primarily to reproduce a known Mission 003 finding;
- or the case cannot be observed without materially reconstructing its already-known outcome.

Entry rejection is not a negative judgment about the Protocol.

It means only that the situation is not appropriate for this Mission's operational evidence relation.

---

# Case Entry Gate

Once a potential operational situation appears, Mission 004 applies the following judgment:

```text
Potential Operational Situation
        ↓
Existing Reasoning State?
        ↓
Reconsideration Trigger?
        ↓
Material Relevance?
        ↓
Genuine Operational Context?
        ↓
========================
    CASE ENTRY GATE
========================
       /        \
     ENTER      DO NOT ENTER
       │             │
       ▼             ▼
03 Observation     Continue normal
Case Definition    reasoning activity
```

Failure to enter Mission 004 does not require the underlying reasoning activity to stop.

It simply remains outside Mission 004 Operational Observation.

---

# Entry Responsibility

The Case Entry Gate has one responsibility:

> **Determine whether the reasoning situation is eligible for Operational Observation under Mission 004.**

It does not determine:

- whether the Protocol is valid;
- whether the Protocol will succeed;
- whether revision will be necessary;
- whether Mission 003 findings will recur;
- whether the case will exercise all mechanisms;
- or what evidence status the Mission will eventually support.

These judgments belong downstream.
