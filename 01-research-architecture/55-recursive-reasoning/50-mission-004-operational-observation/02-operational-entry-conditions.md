# Mission 004 — Operational Entry Conditions

## Entry Conditions for Operational Observation

**Research Area:** Recursive Reasoning  
**Mission:** 004 — Operational Observation  
**Document:** 02 — Operational Entry Conditions  
**Status:** Final — Freeze Candidate  
**Predecessor:** 01-observation-boundary.md  
**Protocol Object:** Frozen Recursive Reasoning Protocol Candidate

---

# Purpose

This document defines the conditions under which a reasoning situation may enter Mission 004 as an Operational Observation Case.

Mission 004 does not require an operational case merely because the Protocol exists and operational evidence remains limited.

A case becomes eligible when a genuine reasoning situation creates a legitimate basis for recursive reconsideration.

The purpose of the Entry Conditions is therefore not to identify a case in which the Protocol is likely to succeed.

The purpose is to determine whether a naturally occurring reasoning situation is structurally appropriate for operational observation.

---

# Core Entry Principle

Mission 004 follows the principle:

> **The Protocol does not create the operational case. The reasoning situation creates the reason for Protocol entry.**

Therefore:

```text
Need to test the Protocol
        ↓
Construct / Select convenient case
        ✗
```

is not a legitimate entry route.

The appropriate route is:

```text
Identifiable Reasoning State
        ↓
Operational Development
        ↓
Possible Need for Reconsideration
        ↓
Entry Conditions
        ↓
Eligible Operational Case?
```

The Entry Conditions protect case legitimacy.

They do not predict Protocol performance.

---

# Four Core Entry Conditions

A reasoning situation becomes eligible for Mission 004 when four conditions are sufficiently present:

```text
1. Identifiable Reasoning State

2. Plausible Reconsideration Trigger

3. Plausible Material Relevance

4. Genuine Operational Context
```

These conditions are structural requirements rather than a numerical scoring system.

They should establish a sufficient basis for operational entry without requiring uncertainty to be artificially eliminated before observation begins.

---

# Condition 1 — Identifiable Reasoning State

Recursive reasoning requires something to which reasoning can meaningfully return.

There should therefore be an identifiable Existing or Working Reasoning State.

This may include:

- a current judgment;
- a provisional conclusion;
- a structural model;
- a research design;
- a candidate architecture;
- an interpretation;
- a decision;
- an operational plan;
- an unresolved but structured line of reasoning;
- a partially developed reasoning path;
- or another sufficiently identifiable reasoning state.

The Reasoning State does not need to be final, formally frozen, or fully stabilized.

A reasoning process still under development may qualify if its current state is sufficiently identifiable that reconsideration can meaningfully return to some part of it.

Therefore:

```text
No identifiable prior or working state
        ≠
Recursive Reconsideration
```

while:

```text
Identifiable Existing / Working State
        +
Possible Need to Revisit It
        →
Recursive Entry Candidate
```

This prevents Mission 004 from restricting Recursive Reasoning only to reconsideration of completed conclusions.

---

# Condition 2 — Plausible Reconsideration Trigger

A development must occur that creates a plausible reason to reconsider the identifiable Reasoning State.

Possible triggers may include:

- a new observation;
- new evidence;
- contradiction;
- unexpected behavior;
- operational failure;
- ambiguity;
- counterexample;
- external challenge;
- changed condition;
- unresolved dependency;
- recognition of an internal inconsistency;
- recognition of an overlooked relation;
- or another development capable of affecting the current reasoning.

The Trigger does not need to originate from a new external input.

It may arise through continued examination of information already present within the reasoning process.

Therefore:

```text
External New Evidence
        → Possible Trigger

Internal Recognition
        → Possible Trigger
```

The Trigger also does not need to establish that revision is necessary.

Its responsibility is narrower:

```text
Trigger
        ≠
Revision Requirement

Trigger
        →
Legitimate Reconsideration Question
```

---

# Condition 3 — Plausible Material Relevance

The Trigger should have a plausible relation to the substance or structure of the identifiable Reasoning State.

Mission 004 does not require Materiality to be conclusively established before operational entry.

Requiring confirmed Materiality would create a circular selection problem:

```text
Materiality is operationally ambiguous
        ↓
Confirmed Materiality required for Entry
        ↓
Case cannot enter
        ↓
Operational Materiality ambiguity
cannot be observed
```

Mission 004 therefore requires:

> **Plausible Material Relevance**

rather than:

> **Pre-confirmed Materiality**

The relevant entry question is:

> **Could this development materially affect the reasoning currently being preserved, developed, or used?**

Trivial wording changes, cosmetic preferences, or developments with no plausible substantive or structural relation to the Reasoning State do not by themselves establish entry.

However, uncertainty about whether a development is materially relevant does not automatically disqualify the case.

If Material Relevance is genuinely uncertain but plausibly present, that uncertainty may itself become part of the operational evidence.

Accordingly:

```text
Clearly No Material Relevance
        → No Entry Basis

Plausible Material Relevance
        → Entry Basis May Exist

Material Relevance Uncertain
but Plausibly Present
        → Uncertainty May Enter Observation
```

Mission 004 does not create a new universal definition or threshold of Materiality.

Materiality already operates within the frozen Protocol and was identified during Mission 003 as an area requiring qualification.

Mission 004 therefore allows its operational behavior to be observed rather than resolving it in advance.

---

# Condition 4 — Genuine Operational Context

The reasoning situation should originate from genuine research, analytical, design, decision, or other substantive reasoning activity.

It should not be created primarily for the purpose of demonstrating or validating the Recursive Reasoning Protocol.

The governing distinction is:

```text
Reasoning activity exists
for its own substantive purpose
        ↓
Recursive reconsideration becomes relevant
        ✓
```

versus:

```text
Need an Operational Observation Case
        ↓
Create or select reasoning activity
primarily to exercise the Protocol
        ✗
```

A Genuine Operational Context does not require that the situation originate outside the current research environment.

It may arise within the same research program in which the Protocol was developed.

The relevant requirement is that the substantive reasoning purpose exists independently of the desire to generate favorable Protocol evidence.

---

# Sufficient Entry Basis

The four Entry Conditions operate together:

```text
Identifiable Reasoning State
        +
Plausible Reconsideration Trigger
        +
Plausible Material Relevance
        +
Genuine Operational Context
        ↓
Sufficient Entry Basis
```

They should not be treated as a rigid checklist requiring artificial certainty at every boundary.

The Entry Judgment may therefore distinguish among:

```text
SUFFICIENTLY ELIGIBLE
        ↓
ENTER

CLEARLY INELIGIBLE
        ↓
DO NOT ENTER

MATERIALLY UNCERTAIN
        ↓
Preserve the uncertainty
rather than manufacture certainty
```

Material uncertainty does not automatically authorize entry.

Nor does it automatically prohibit entry.

The relevant question is whether sufficient evidence exists to justify treating the situation as a legitimate operational opportunity while preserving the uncertainty itself.

---

# Entry Does Not Predict Outcome

Eligibility for Mission 004 does not imply that:

- the Protocol will operate smoothly;
- revision will occur;
- preservation will occur;
- every Protocol mechanism will be exercised;
- the Protocol will resolve the reasoning problem;
- or the resulting evidence will support the Protocol.

Therefore:

```text
Eligible Operational Case
        ≠
Successful Protocol Case
```

Case eligibility concerns the legitimacy of observation.

Protocol behavior remains an open operational question.

---

# Mechanism Coverage Is Not an Entry Requirement

An operational case does not need to exercise every Protocol mechanism.

A case may legitimately exercise only part of the Protocol.

For example:

```text
Trigger / Re-entry        EXERCISED
Target / Depth            EXERCISED
Preserve / Revise         EXERCISED
Restabilization           EXERCISED
Recursive Return          NOT EXERCISED
```

This remains a valid operational observation.

Mission 004 should not prefer cases merely because they appear likely to exercise more Protocol components.

Mechanisms not exercised by the case should later be recorded as:

> **NOT EXERCISED**

rather than treated as supported or refuted.

---

# Known-Finding Protection

Mission 003 identified known Revision Candidates concerning:

```text
Materiality

Preserve → Restabilization

Recursive-return Routing
```

These known findings do not themselves establish Mission 004 entry.

A case should not be selected because it appears likely to reproduce one of these findings.

Therefore:

```text
Known Revision Candidate
        ↓
Search for matching case
        ✗
```

However, if an otherwise eligible operational case naturally encounters one of these issues, the event may be preserved under the Observation Boundary.

Known findings should neither be actively reproduced nor artificially suppressed.

---

# Retrospective Case Protection

A reasoning episode whose outcome is already known should not ordinarily be reconstructed and presented as if it were a live Operational Observation Case.

Historical cases may have research value.

They create a different evidence relation.

Mission 004 prioritizes prospective or sufficiently contemporaneous observation in which operational behavior can be preserved without reconstructing the case around its known outcome.

Therefore:

```text
Natural Situation
        ↓
Entry Judgment
        ↓
Operational Observation
        ✓
```

is preferred over:

```text
Known Historical Outcome
        ↓
Retrospective Reconstruction
        ↓
Presented as Live Operational Observation
        ✗
```

Historical material may later be used under an explicitly different evidence classification if justified.

---

# Natural Does Not Mean Accidental

A Natural Operational Case does not need to occur unexpectedly.

The research environment may deliberately remain prepared to recognize situations in which recursive reconsideration becomes relevant.

What should not be predetermined is the case outcome or the need to demonstrate Protocol success.

Therefore:

```text
Prepared to recognize
a legitimate operational opportunity
        ✓

Manufacture an opportunity
to produce Protocol evidence
        ✗
```

Mission 004 may therefore remain operationally ready without turning case discovery into case construction.

---

# Operational Naturalness

Entry assessment should remain sufficiently lightweight that the process of determining eligibility does not materially restructure the reasoning situation itself.

Mission 004 should not require the underlying reasoning activity to stop and become an artificial test environment merely to satisfy cleaner entry documentation.

The governing principle inherited from the Observation Boundary is:

> **Preserve evidential distinguishability without materially distorting operational behavior.**

Therefore:

```text
Recognize Operational Opportunity
        ↓
Preserve sufficient Entry Evidence
        ↓
Continue genuine reasoning activity
        ✓
```

rather than:

```text
Recognize possible opportunity
        ↓
Restructure reasoning activity
around Mission 004 requirements
        ↓
Create cleaner Case
        ✗
```

This protects the naturalness of the operational situation while preserving sufficient evidence for later assessment.

---

# Human–AI Context

A reasoning situation occurring within Human–AI dialogue may satisfy the Entry Conditions.

Human–AI dialogue is neither automatically eligible nor automatically excluded.

The same four conditions apply:

```text
Identifiable Reasoning State
        +
Plausible Reconsideration Trigger
        +
Plausible Material Relevance
        +
Genuine Operational Context
```

If the case occurs within a Human–AI relationship historically connected to Protocol formation, that relationship should later be recorded as part of the evidence context.

Such a case may produce operational evidence without automatically producing independent evidence.

---

# Entry Rejection

A candidate situation should not enter Mission 004 when:

- no identifiable Existing or Working Reasoning State exists;
- no plausible Reconsideration Trigger exists;
- the Trigger has no plausible material relation to the Reasoning State;
- the situation was created primarily to demonstrate the Protocol;
- the case is selected primarily because a favorable result is expected;
- the case is selected primarily to reproduce a known Mission 003 finding;
- or the case cannot be observed without materially reconstructing its already-known outcome.

Entry rejection is not a negative judgment about the Protocol.

It means only that the situation is not appropriate for this Mission's operational evidence relation.

---

# Case Entry Gate

Once a potential operational situation appears, Mission 004 applies the following judgment:

```text
Potential Operational Situation
        ↓
Identifiable Reasoning State?
        ↓
Plausible Reconsideration Trigger?
        ↓
Plausible Material Relevance?
        ↓
Genuine Operational Context?
        ↓
Sufficient Entry Basis?
        ↓
========================
    CASE ENTRY GATE
========================
       /        \
     ENTER      DO NOT ENTER
       │             │
       ▼             ▼
03 Observation     Continue normal
Case Definition    reasoning activity
```

Where material uncertainty remains, the uncertainty should be preserved rather than silently resolved merely to produce an Entry decision.

Failure to enter Mission 004 does not require the underlying reasoning activity to stop.

It simply remains outside Mission 004 Operational Observation.

---

# Entry Responsibility

The Case Entry Gate has one responsibility:

> **Determine whether the reasoning situation has a sufficient and legitimate basis for Operational Observation under Mission 004.**

It does not determine:

- whether the Protocol is valid;
- whether the Protocol will succeed;
- whether revision will be necessary;
- whether Mission 003 findings will recur;
- whether the case will exercise all mechanisms;
- or what evidence status the Mission will eventually support.

These judgments belong downstream.

---

# Counter-test Status

The Entry Conditions were subjected to a Strong Counter-test against the following challenge:

> **Do the Entry Conditions function as a selection filter favoring Protocol-compatible cases, or exclude important cases in which Recursive Reasoning is genuinely required?**

The Counter-test identified three principal risks:

1. interpreting Existing Reasoning State too narrowly as a completed reasoning product;
2. requiring confirmed Materiality before operational entry;
3. applying the four conditions as a rigid certainty-based checklist.

These risks were repaired by:

- explicitly including identifiable Working Reasoning States;
- replacing confirmed Materiality with Plausible Material Relevance;
- allowing material uncertainty to remain visible at Entry;
- and defining the four conditions as a sufficient structural basis rather than a numerical or certainty-based filter.

The four-condition architecture survived the Counter-test.

**Counter-test Disposition: TARGETED REVISION → PRESERVE**
