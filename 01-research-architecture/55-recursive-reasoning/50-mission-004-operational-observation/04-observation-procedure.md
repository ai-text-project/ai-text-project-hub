# Purpose

This document defines the case-neutral procedure by which Mission 004 will observe and preserve the operational behavior of the frozen Recursive Reasoning Protocol.

The procedure is designed before a specific operational case is selected.

This ordering protects Mission 004 against adapting the observation procedure to the characteristics, expected outcome, or known difficulties of a particular case.

The responsibility of this document is:

> **to define how operational behavior will be observed and preserved once an eligible case enters Mission 004.**

It does not define how the Recursive Reasoning Protocol itself should behave.

It does not replace, repair, extend, or reinterpret the frozen Protocol Object.

---

# Procedural Boundary

Mission 004 distinguishes between:

```text
Protocol
What reasoning responsibilities exist?
        ↓
Observation Procedure
How is their operational behavior observed and preserved?
```

Therefore:

```text
Observation Procedure
        ≠
Protocol Execution Specification
```

The Observation Procedure must not introduce a new canonical sequence, decision rule, threshold, routing rule, or responsibility that is absent from the frozen Protocol.

If operational use requires interpretation because the Protocol itself is ambiguous, that interpretation belongs to the operational evidence.

It should not be silently converted into a procedural rule by this document.

---

# Case-Neutrality Principle

This procedure is defined without reference to the substantive content or expected behavior of Case 001.

It should therefore remain applicable across eligible operational cases without assuming:

- a particular research topic;
- a particular Trigger;
- a particular recursive target;
- a particular depth;
- Preserve or Revise as the expected outcome;
- successful Restabilization;
- Recursive Return;
- activation of every Protocol mechanism;
- recurrence of Mission 003 findings;
- or successful completion of the underlying reasoning task.

The governing principle is:

> **Define the observation procedure before knowing what the case will show.**

# Observation Object

The primary observation object is:

> **the behavior of the frozen Recursive Reasoning Protocol in relation to an eligible operational reasoning situation.**

Observation may include behavior associated with:

- Trigger / Re-entry;
- Recursive Target;
- Recursive Depth;
- Preserve / Revise;
- Restabilization;
- Recursive Return;
- Human–AI responsibility distribution;
- ambiguity;
- friction;
- non-exercise;
- operational impasse;
- unexpected behavior;
- and reasoning behavior not explicitly represented by the Protocol.

These are observation dimensions.

They are not required stages that every case must exercise.

---

# Core Procedure

Mission 004 uses the following case-neutral observation sequence:

```text
CASE ENTRY
        ↓
INITIAL STATE PRESERVATION
        ↓
FROZEN PROTOCOL APPLICATION
        ↓
OPERATIONAL EVENT PRESERVATION
        ↓
MECHANISM / RESPONSIBILITY TRACKING
        ↓
CASE TERMINATION
        ↓
RAW OBSERVATION CLOSURE
        ↓
BEHAVIOR ASSESSMENT
```

The sequence organizes evidence responsibility.

It should not be interpreted as requiring artificial interruption of the underlying reasoning activity at every stage.

---

# Step 1 — Case Entry Confirmation

Before operational observation begins, confirm that the case has passed the Mission 004 Case Entry Gate and has been defined in:

`03-observation-case-definition.md`

The confirmation should establish that the case has a sufficient basis involving:

```text
Identifiable Reasoning State

Plausible Reconsideration Trigger

Plausible Material Relevance

Genuine Operational Context
```

The Observation Procedure should not reopen case selection merely because the subsequent operational behavior appears unfavorable.

Once validly entered, a difficult or unsuccessful case remains evidence.

---

# Step 2 — Initial State Preservation

Before recursive reconsideration materially changes the reasoning state, preserve enough information to identify the state from which reconsideration begins.

The Initial State may include:

- the relevant reasoning position;
- current judgment or working conclusion;
- relevant structural assumptions;
- unresolved questions;
- current decision or design;
- known evidence relevant to the case;
- and the development that created the reconsideration question.

The objective is not exhaustive documentation.

The objective is to preserve enough of the pre-recursive state to make later change, preservation, or return observable.

Therefore:

```text
Sufficient Initial State
        ✓

Exhaustive reconstruction
that materially interrupts reasoning
        ✗
```

---

# Step 3 — Frozen Protocol Application

The operational case should use the frozen Recursive Reasoning Protocol Candidate as the Protocol Object.

No silent modification is permitted.

The Observation Procedure does not prescribe how every Protocol judgment must be made.

Instead, it observes how the frozen Protocol is actually used.

Possible operational outcomes include:

```text
Protocol responsibility applied clearly

Protocol responsibility applied with interpretation

Protocol responsibility ambiguous

Protocol responsibility not exercised

Protocol responsibility insufficient for the situation

External operational judgment required

Operational continuation impossible
```

Each is potentially valid evidence.

---

# Step 4 — Operational Event Preservation

Operationally relevant events should be preserved as close as reasonably possible to their occurrence.

Examples include:

- recognition of a possible Trigger;
- Re-entry judgment;
- identification or uncertainty of Target;
- Depth judgment;
- Preserve / Revise judgment;
- Restabilization attempt;
- Recursive Return;
- ambiguity;
- friction;
- external judgment;
- unexpected behavior;
- and inability to continue.

Preservation should distinguish, where reasonably possible:

```text
What occurred

What was observed

What was interpreted during operation
```

These responsibilities may occur close together in time.

They need not be artificially separated during live reasoning.

---

# Step 5 — Preserve Operational Naturalness

Evidence preservation must not materially distort the behavior being observed.

The procedure should therefore remain lightweight during active reasoning.

The governing principle is:

> **Preserve evidential distinguishability without materially distorting operational behavior.**

Mission 004 should avoid an observation pattern such as:

```text
Reason
  ↓
Stop
  ↓
Document extensively
  ↓
Restart reasoning
  ↓
Stop
  ↓
Document extensively
```

when that pattern would materially change the reasoning process itself.

Instead:

```text
Reason naturally
        ↓
Preserve material events
at the nearest reasonable point
        ↓
Continue reasoning
```

is preferred.

If preservation is delayed to protect operational naturalness, the delayed nature of the record should remain identifiable.

---

# Step 6 — Track Protocol Mechanisms Without Forcing Them

Mission 004 should track which Protocol responsibilities become operationally relevant.

Possible statuses include:

```text
EXERCISED

EXERCISED WITH INTERPRETATION

AMBIGUOUS

FRICTION OBSERVED

NOT EXERCISED

EXTERNAL JUDGMENT REQUIRED

OPERATIONALLY BLOCKING
```

These statuses support later assessment.

They do not themselves determine Protocol validity.

No mechanism should be artificially activated merely to increase observation coverage.

Therefore:

```text
Mechanism naturally relevant
        → Observe

Mechanism not naturally relevant
        → NOT EXERCISED
```

---

# Step 7 — Preserve Interpretation Without Converting It into Protocol

Operational reasoning may require interpretation.

This is expected.

For example, a Human or AI participant may need to interpret:

- whether a Trigger is materially relevant;
- what the recursive target should be;
- how deeply reconsideration should proceed;
- whether preservation is justified;
- whether sufficient Restabilization has occurred;
- or where reasoning should return.

Such interpretation may be necessary for genuine Protocol use.

It should therefore be preserved rather than prohibited.

However:

```text
Operational Interpretation
        ≠
Canonical Protocol Rule
```

If interpretation is required because the Protocol does not determine the judgment clearly, the interpretation itself becomes part of the operational evidence.

The Observation Procedure must not silently formalize that interpretation as if it had already existed in the frozen Protocol.

---

# Step 8 — Preserve External Operational Judgment

A case may require reasoning that is not explicitly represented by the Protocol.

If this occurs, Mission 004 should preserve the event.

Possible classification:

> **EXTERNAL OPERATIONAL JUDGMENT REQUIRED**

This does not automatically mean that the Protocol failed.

It may indicate:

- an intentional Protocol boundary;
- an unspecified responsibility;
- an operational dependency;
- a case-specific requirement;
- or a possible structural omission.

The appropriate interpretation belongs downstream.

The operational record should first preserve that the external judgment occurred and how it related to continued reasoning.

---

# Step 9 — Known-Finding Neutrality

Mission 003 identified Revision Candidates concerning:

```text
Materiality

Preserve → Restabilization explicitness

Recursive-return Routing
```

The Observation Procedure should not actively search for these findings during operational use.

It should also not suppress them.

Therefore:

```text
Known Finding
        ↓
Naturally becomes operationally relevant?
       / \
     YES  NO
      │    │
      ▼    ▼
   Record  Do not infer
```

Correspondence with Mission 003 should be assessed only after the relevant operational event has been preserved.

This protects the distinction between:

```text
Operational Observation
        ≠
Known-Finding Confirmation Search
```

---

# Step 10 — Preserve Non-Exercise

If a Protocol mechanism or responsibility does not become relevant during the case, record:

> **NOT EXERCISED**

Do not infer:

- operational support;
- operational failure;
- absence of ambiguity;
- or resolution of a known qualification.

Non-exercise is an observation status, not an evaluation.

---

# Step 11 — Operational Impasse

Operational observation may encounter a point at which continued use of the frozen Protocol becomes difficult or impossible.

When this occurs:

```text
Operational Impasse
        ↓
Preserve the event
        ↓
Can genuine reasoning continue
without modifying the Protocol?
       / \
     YES  NO
      │    │
      ▼    ▼
 Continue  Terminate
```

If reasoning continues through an external judgment, interpretation, or other non-Protocol resource, that dependency should be preserved.

If continued observation would require modifying the frozen Protocol, the case may terminate.

Termination is a legitimate operational result.

---

# Step 12 — No Mid-observation Protocol Repair

Mission 004 does not repair the Protocol during operational observation merely because friction, ambiguity, or failure appears.

Therefore:

```text
Friction
        ↓
Record
        ✓

Friction
        ↓
Modify Protocol
        ↓
Continue as if unchanged
        ✗
```

Ideas for possible improvement may arise during operation.

They may be preserved separately as later Revision Candidates.

They should not alter the Protocol Object being observed in the current case.

---

# Step 13 — Case Termination

An Operational Observation Case may terminate when:

- recursive reconsideration has reached a sufficiently stable operational endpoint;
- the underlying reasoning activity legitimately ends;
- the Protocol reaches an operational impasse;
- continuation would require modifying the frozen Protocol;
- the operational situation materially changes beyond the defined Case Boundary;
- or another case-specific termination condition defined in `03` is reached.

Termination does not require:

- successful Protocol performance;
- revision;
- preservation;
- activation of all mechanisms;
- or successful resolution of the underlying substantive problem.

The termination condition and reason should be preserved.

---

# Step 14 — Raw Observation Closure

After Case Termination, the operational record should be closed before Protocol-level interpretation is finalized.

The Raw Observation should preserve, as applicable:

- Initial Reasoning State;
- Entry basis;
- relevant operational events;
- Protocol responsibilities exercised;
- interpretations required;
- ambiguities;
- friction;
- external judgments;
- non-exercised mechanisms;
- unexpected observations;
- impasses;
- termination condition;
- and preservation timing where relevant.

The purpose is to establish an inspectable evidence layer before downstream assessment.

---

# Procedure Failure Protections

The Procedure protects against at least the following failure modes.

## Protocol Substitution

The Observation Procedure becomes a more detailed replacement for the frozen Protocol.

## Case-specific Procedure Adaptation

Observation rules are modified after seeing what the case is likely to show.

## Observer Distortion

Evidence collection materially changes the operational reasoning behavior.

## Mechanism Forcing

Protocol mechanisms are deliberately activated to increase observation coverage.

## Interpretation Formalization

Case-specific interpretation is silently converted into a Protocol rule.

## Mid-observation Repair

Protocol friction is repaired during the case and the modified behavior is treated as behavior of the frozen Protocol.

## Known-Finding Search Bias

Mission 003 findings become targets that the operational case is expected to reproduce.

## Non-exercise Inflation

Unexercised mechanisms are treated as operationally supported.

## Outcome-oriented Termination

The case is continued or terminated primarily to obtain a favorable Protocol result.

## Evidence–Assessment Mixing

Protocol-level conclusions are inserted into the raw operational record as though they were direct observations.

# Counter-test Status

This Procedure was subjected to a Strong Counter-test before externalization.

The principal challenge was:

> **Does the Observation Procedure become so detailed that it silently supplies operational rules absent from the frozen Protocol, causing Mission 004 to observe a procedure-enhanced Protocol rather than the actual frozen Protocol?**

A second material challenge concerned observer distortion:

> **Could evidence-preservation requirements alter the natural reasoning behavior that Mission 004 intends to observe?**

The Counter-test identified both risks as material.

They were repaired by:

- defining the Procedure as an evidence-preservation procedure rather than a Protocol execution specification;
- prohibiting new canonical decision or routing rules;
- allowing necessary operational interpretation while preserving it as evidence;
- explicitly recording external operational judgments;
- keeping live observation lightweight;
- and permitting delayed preservation where contemporaneous recording would materially distort operational behavior.

After repair, the case-neutral Procedure remained coherent with the Mission Definition, Observation Boundary, and Operational Entry Conditions.

**Counter-test Disposition: TARGETED REVISION → PRESERVE**
