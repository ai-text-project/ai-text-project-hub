# Purpose

This document defines the case-neutral architecture for converting an eligible naturally occurring operational reasoning situation into a bounded Mission 004 Observation Case.

Its responsibility is:

> **to define what operational situation is being observed, where the Case Boundary lies, what reasoning state and reconsideration basis existed at entry, and what remains outside the case, without manufacturing, simplifying, or reshaping the substantive reasoning situation for the purpose of Protocol observation.**

This document is designed before Case 001 exists.

It therefore defines how a future eligible case will be bounded and instantiated.

It does not define the substantive content of Case 001 in advance.

---

# Core Distinction

Mission 004 distinguishes between:

```text
Case Definition Architecture
        ↓
Defines how an eligible natural case
will be bounded and preserved

Case-specific Definition
        ↓
Can exist only after an eligible
natural operational situation exists
```

Therefore:

```text
Predefined Case Definition Architecture
        ✓

Predefined Case Content
        ✗
```

The architecture may exist before Case 001.

Case 001 itself may not.

---

# Central Case Definition Question

The central question is:

> **What naturally occurring operational reasoning situation has legitimately entered Mission 004, and what is the minimum defensible boundary within which its Protocol-relevant behavior can be observed?**

This differs from asking:

> What would make a good Protocol test?

or:

> What case would exercise the most Protocol mechanisms?

or:

> What case would confirm or challenge Mission 003 findings?

Those questions must not determine Case Definition.

---

Its governing relation is:

```text
Identifiable Reasoning State
+
Plausible Reconsideration Trigger
+
Plausible Material Relevance
+

        ↓
Sufficient Entry Basis
```

03 does not replace this judgment.

03 begins only after an operational situation has reached:

```text

        ↓

```

Therefore:

```text

        ↓

```

03 must not weaken 02 merely to obtain a usable case.

---

# Case Definition Is Not Case Creation

The most important boundary is:

> **03 defines a Case that already has a legitimate operational basis. It does not create that basis.**

Therefore:

```text
Natural Operational Situation
        ↓
Entry Eligibility
        ↓
Case Definition
```

not:

```text
Need for Mission 004 Evidence
        ↓
Construct Interesting Situation
        ↓
Call It a Case
```

A case may be recognized.

A case may be bounded.

A case may be described.

A case must not be manufactured merely because Mission 004 requires operational evidence.

---

# Natural Does Not Mean Accidental

A naturally occurring case need not appear without preparation or recognition.

Mission 004 may remain ready to identify eligible situations.

Researchers may recognize that a substantive reasoning situation satisfies the Entry Conditions.

However:

> **Readiness to recognize a case must not become pressure to create one.**

Therefore:

```text
Prepared Recognition
        ✓

Evidence-seeking Manufacture
        ✗
```

---

# Genuine Operational Context

Every instantiated Observation Case must arise from a substantive activity that exists for a reason other than producing favorable Mission 004 evidence.

Examples may include:

- research architecture design;
- methodology development;
- evidence interpretation;
- repository restructuring;
- comparative analysis;
- substantive writing;
- decision-making;
- conceptual integration;
- or another genuine reasoning task.

The substantive activity may occur within the same broader research program.

Externality is not required.

What is required is:

> **the reasoning situation must have a genuine substantive purpose independent of the desire to generate a Mission 004 case.**

# Existing State Protection

Do not retrospectively make the prior state appear more stable, explicit, or complete than it actually was.

For example:

```text

        ✓

Later reconstructed formal doctrine
        ✗
```

Mission 004 observes recursive reasoning from the state that genuinely existed.

It does not improve the prior state for analytical convenience.

# 03 — Reconsideration Trigger

Identify the event, observation, conflict, question, discrepancy, or other development that created a plausible reason for reconsideration.

Possible Trigger forms may include:

```text
External Evidence

Internal Contradiction

Unexpected Observation

Boundary Conflict

Operational Friction

New Comparison

User Challenge

AI-generated Counter-consideration

Human Recognition

Joint Human–AI Recognition

Other Substantive Development
```

These are examples only.

No Trigger type is required.

---

# Trigger Preservation

Record:

```text
Trigger:

Source:

Timing:

Relationship to Existing State:

Why Reconsideration Became Plausible:
```

Do not rewrite a weak or ambiguous Trigger into a stronger one after the case outcome is known.

If the Trigger was uncertain, preserve the uncertainty.

---

# Trigger Is Not Yet Materiality

The presence of a Trigger does not establish that revision is necessary.

Likewise:

```text
Trigger
        ≠
Material Problem

Reconsideration
        ≠
Revision

Unexpected Observation
        ≠
Protocol Failure
```

At Case Definition, only plausible reconsideration relevance is required.

---

# 04 — Plausible Material Relevance

Record why the Trigger could plausibly matter to the current reasoning state.

The standard is not:

> Materiality has already been established.

The standard is:

> **There is sufficient plausible material relevance to justify recursive reconsideration.**

Possible outcomes include:

```text
Plausibly Material

Materiality Uncertain but Legitimately Assessable

Apparently Minor but Potentially Structural

Insufficiently Clear for Stronger Classification
```

Do not require the future conclusion to be known at entry.

---

# Materiality Protection

Case Definition must not transform:

```text
Plausible Material Relevance
```

into:

```text
Confirmed Materiality
```

merely to justify Mission entry.

Otherwise the Case Definition would pre-decide one of the questions that operational reasoning may need to resolve.

# 06 — Observation Object

Define what is actually being observed.

The Observation Object should ordinarily be expressed as:

> **the behavior of the frozen Recursive Reasoning Protocol within the bounded substantive reasoning situation.**

It is not:

- the substantive topic as a whole;
- the quality of the Human participant;
- the quality of the AI participant;
- the entire research project;
- the success of the substantive task;
- or the Protocol's general validity.

Record:

```text
Observation Object:

Relevant Protocol Responsibilities:

Responsibilities Not Yet Known to Be Relevant:
```

Do not require every Protocol responsibility to become relevant.

---

# Mechanism Exposure Must Not Be Engineered

A Case should not be expanded merely to exercise more Protocol mechanisms.

For example:

```text
Natural Case exercises:
Trigger
Target / Depth
Preserve

Recursive Return does not arise
```

The correct result is:

```text
Recursive Return
→ NOT EXERCISED
```

not:

```text
Extend Case
→ Create opportunity for Recursive Return
```

Case Definition must preserve natural mechanism exposure.

# 07 — Case Boundary

Define the minimum defensible boundary of the operational case.

The boundary should identify:

```text
What is inside the case?

What is outside the case?

What reasoning state marks the relevant beginning?

What substantive activity remains relevant?

What later activity would constitute a different case?
```

The governing principle is:

> **Use the smallest boundary that preserves the materially relevant operational reasoning without stripping away context necessary to understand Protocol behavior.**

---

# Minimum Defensible Boundary

Two opposite failures must be avoided.

## Boundary Too Narrow

```text
Trigger only
        ↓
Relevant prior state lost
        ↓
Protocol behavior becomes uninterpretable
```

## Boundary Too Broad

```text
Entire research conversation
        ↓
Unrelated reasoning included
        ↓
Case becomes analytically unbounded
```

Therefore:

```text
Necessary Context
+
Material Operational Sequence
        ↓
Minimum Defensible Case Boundary
```

---

# Boundary Is Evidential

Case Boundary affects what later becomes observable evidence.

Therefore, boundary selection must remain inspectable.

Record:

```text
Boundary Start:

Boundary End Principle:

Included Context:

Excluded Context:

Reason for Boundary:
```

If the boundary is uncertain, preserve that uncertainty rather than forcing artificial precision.

# 10 — Observation Start

Define when Mission 004 operational observation formally begins.

This should occur after:

```text
Eligible Natural Situation
        ↓
Entry Decision
        ↓
Case Definition
        ↓
Observation Start
```

The Observation Start should be identifiable enough to distinguish:

- pre-case context;
- operational observation;
- and later assessment.

---

# Observation Start Does Not Require Artificial Interruption

The substantive reasoning situation should not be materially distorted merely to create a formal Observation Start.

If the case is recognized while reasoning is already underway:

```text
Recognition occurs
        ↓
Case is bounded
        ↓
Observation begins with minimal disruption
```

Relevant immediately preceding context may be preserved as near-contemporaneous evidence where justified.

---

# Retrospective Case Recognition

A case may sometimes be recognized only after relevant reasoning has already begun.

This does not automatically invalidate the case.

However, distinguish:

```text
Near-contemporaneous recognition
        ✓ potentially usable

Retrospective reconstruction after outcome known
        ⚠ evidentially qualified

Historical reconstruction primarily from memory
        ✗ not equivalent to live operational observation
```

Any retrospective element must remain explicit.

Mission 004 must not present reconstructed history as contemporaneously observed behavior.

---

# 11 — Anticipated Boundary Conditions

Before observation begins, record any known limitations that may affect interpretation.

Examples include:

```text
Partial evidence preservation

Pre-existing knowledge of Mission 003 findings

Human–AI role asymmetry

Substantive time constraints

Incomplete context

Possible Observer Effect

Ambiguous Protocol Entry Point

Other known operational limitation
```

These conditions do not automatically disqualify a case.

They establish evidential context.

---

# No Expected Outcome Field

The Case Definition should not contain fields such as:

```text
Expected Protocol Result

Expected Revision

Expected Success

Expected Failure

Expected Mission 003 Confirmation
```

Such fields create unnecessary anticipatory pressure on later observation.

If a substantive prediction genuinely exists independently of Mission 004, it may be preserved as context.

It should not become an Observation target merely because it exists.

# Case Definition Completeness

A valid Case Definition does not require perfect information.

It requires sufficient definition to establish:

```text
What substantive situation exists?

What prior reasoning state exists?

Why reconsideration became plausible?

Why the situation legitimately entered Mission 004?

What exactly is being observed?

Where does the Case Boundary lie?

When does operational observation begin?
```

If these cannot be established sufficiently, operational observation should not proceed as a fully eligible Mission 004 case.

---

# Case Definition and Protocol Neutrality

03 must not silently add requirements to the frozen Protocol.

Its responsibility is observational architecture.

Therefore, Case Definition must not establish:

- new Trigger thresholds;
- new Materiality thresholds;
- new Target-selection rules;
- new Depth rules;
- new Preserve / Revise rules;
- new Restabilization criteria;
- new Recursive-return routing;
- or new Human–AI responsibility assignments.

If such operational interpretation becomes necessary, it belongs to the observed case and should later be preserved as evidence.

---

# Case Definition and Research Neutrality

Complete neutrality is not assumed.

The Human and AI participants may know:

- the Protocol;
- the Mission;
- Mission 003 findings;
- and the purpose of Mission 004.

Mission 004 therefore does not claim observer-free naturalism.

Its narrower responsibility is:

> **to prevent the Case Definition Architecture from materially manufacturing or reshaping the substantive reasoning situation merely to obtain preferred Protocol evidence.**

---

# Case Selection Boundary

Mission 004 should not rank natural cases according to how likely they are to produce interesting Protocol findings.

A case may legitimately be:

- simple;
- partial;
- ambiguous;
- operationally unsuccessful;
- mechanism-limited;
- or evidentially weak.

If it satisfies Entry Conditions, its evidential limitations can be preserved downstream.

Interestingness is not an Entry Condition.

---

# Case Rejection

A candidate situation should not enter if, for example:

```text
No identifiable prior reasoning state exists

No plausible reconsideration Trigger exists

Material relevance is purely manufactured

The substantive task exists only to test the Protocol

The case is primarily retrospective reconstruction

The boundary cannot be established sufficiently

Protocol use would itself create the substantive problem
```

Rejection of a candidate case is not Mission failure.

---

# Case Hold

Some situations may be plausible but insufficiently clear for immediate entry.

In such cases:

```text
Candidate Situation
        ↓
Material Uncertainty
        ↓
HOLD
```

may be preferable to:

```text
Candidate Situation
        ↓
Pressure for Case 001
        ↓
Artificial ENTER
```

A Hold does not require active monitoring or intervention.

The substantive activity may continue naturally.

# Case Termination Boundary

03 defines the principle for identifying when the bounded case should cease to be treated as the same Observation Case.

Possible termination bases may later include:

- substantive reasoning state sufficiently resolved;
- Protocol use no longer materially relevant;
- operational impasse;
- transition into a materially different reasoning problem;
- evidence preservation becomes insufficient;
- or another case-specific boundary.

03 does not predefine the actual outcome.

04 governs observation procedure and later termination handling.

---

# Multiple Recursive Episodes

A substantive task may contain more than one recursive episode.

Do not automatically assume:

```text
One substantive task
=
One Observation Case
```

or:

```text
Every recursive return
=
New Observation Case
```

Case separation should depend on whether the episodes remain part of the same materially coherent operational reasoning situation.

If uncertain, preserve the boundary question for later assessment rather than forcing artificial segmentation.

---

# Nested Reasoning Protection

Recursive reasoning may itself produce new reasoning questions.

This does not automatically require nested Mission 004 cases.

Otherwise:

```text
Case
    ↓
Recursive Question
    ↓
New Case
        ↓
Recursive Question
            ↓
New Case
```

could produce uncontrolled observational recursion.

The default is:

> **Keep recursively generated reasoning within the existing Case Boundary when it remains materially part of the same substantive reconsideration.**

A new case requires a separately defensible operational boundary.

# Case Definition Integrity

Once Observation Case Definition is closed, it should not be rewritten after the operational result becomes known merely to make the case:

- cleaner;
- more coherent;
- more successful;
- more Protocol-relevant;
- more consistent with Mission 003;
- or easier to assess.

Corrections of factual error may be made transparently.

Outcome-driven reconstruction is prohibited.

---

# Failure Protections

The Case Definition Architecture protects against at least the following failure modes.

## Case Manufacture

A substantive problem is created primarily to obtain Mission 004 evidence.

## Evidence-seeking Selection

Cases are selected because they are expected to confirm or challenge known Protocol findings.

## Boundary Engineering

Case boundaries are chosen to maximize Protocol-relevant evidence.

## Mechanism Engineering

The case is extended or altered to exercise additional Protocol responsibilities.

## Materiality Inflation

Plausible material relevance is rewritten as confirmed Materiality at entry.

## Prior-state Reconstruction

The pre-existing reasoning state is retrospectively formalized beyond what actually existed.

## Trigger Inflation

An ambiguous event is rewritten as a strong reconsideration Trigger after the outcome is known.

## Known-Finding Contamination

Mission 003 findings determine Case selection or boundary.

## Retrospective Case Creation

A completed historical reasoning episode is reconstructed and presented as live operational observation.

## Boundary Expansion

Interesting later events are silently absorbed into the original Case.

## Nested-case Proliferation

Every recursive episode is converted into a separate Observation Case.

## Protocol Supplementation

Case Definition silently introduces operational rules absent from the frozen Protocol.

## Outcome-driven Editing

Case Definition is rewritten after observation to fit the eventual assessment.

# Counter-test Status

This Case Definition Architecture was subjected to a Strong Counter-test before externalization.

The principal challenge was:

> **Does defining the Case Definition Architecture before Case 001 exists cause a future naturally occurring reasoning situation to be selected, bounded, or reshaped according to what would make it a useful Protocol observation case?**

The Counter-test identified a material risk of Case Manufacture and Boundary Engineering.

A second challenge was:

> **Could the act of defining a Case transform plausible reconsideration into assumed Materiality, thereby pre-deciding the recursive reasoning question before Protocol observation begins?**

A third challenge was:

> **Could knowledge of Mission 003 Revision Candidates cause future Case Definition to preferentially isolate situations likely to reproduce those known findings?**

A fourth challenge was:

> **Could a fixed Case Definition template encourage retrospective completion of prior state, Trigger, boundary, or entry information after the case outcome is known?**

These risks were repaired by:

- defining 03 as Case Boundary Architecture rather than Case Creation Architecture;
- requiring 02 Entry Eligibility before 03 instantiation;
- preserving Genuine Operational Context as independent of Mission 004 evidence production;
- distinguishing plausible Material Relevance from confirmed Materiality;
- using the Minimum Defensible Case Boundary principle;
- prohibiting mechanism engineering and evidence-seeking case expansion;
- making known Mission 003 findings visible context rather than selection criteria;
- distinguishing Case Boundary Start from Protocol Entry Point;
- allowing uncertainty and missing information to remain explicit;
- protecting against retrospective reconstruction;
- requiring Case Definition Closure before operational outcome is known;
- and treating later boundary modification as observable evidence rather than silent correction.

After repair, the Case Definition Architecture can be frozen before Case 001 without manufacturing or predetermining the substantive operational case.

**Counter-test Disposition: TARGETED REVISION → PRESERVE**
