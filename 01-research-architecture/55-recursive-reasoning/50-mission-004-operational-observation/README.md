# Mission 004 — Operational Observation

## Overview

Mission 004 observes how the frozen Recursive Reasoning Protocol behaves when applied to a naturally occurring operational reasoning situation.

The Mission does not begin by attempting to validate the Protocol.

Its responsibility is to preserve and assess what actually occurs when the Protocol becomes relevant within genuine substantive reasoning activity.

The central question is:

> **How does the frozen Recursive Reasoning Protocol behave when applied to a naturally occurring operational reasoning situation?**
