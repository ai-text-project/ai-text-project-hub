# Mission 004 — Operational Observation

## Mission Definition

**Research Area:** Recursive Reasoning  
**Mission:** 004  
**Mission Type:** Operational Observation  
**Status:** Active — Mission Definition  
**Protocol Object:** Frozen Recursive Reasoning Protocol Candidate  
**Predecessor:** Mission 003 — Independent Examination

---

# Purpose

Mission 004 examines how the frozen Recursive Reasoning Protocol behaves when applied to a naturally occurring operational reasoning situation.

The Protocol Candidate was formed during Mission 002 and independently examined during Mission 003.

Mission 003 concluded with the following status:

```text
EXAMINATION STATUS
→ INDEPENDENTLY EXAMINED

EVIDENCE STATUS
→ QUALIFIED EVIDENCE SUPPORT

PROTOCOL DISPOSITION
→ QUALIFIED PRESERVE
```

Mission 004 begins from that preserved state.

Its responsibility is not to further redesign the Protocol before use.

Its responsibility is to observe the operational behavior of the preserved Protocol when it is applied to an actual reasoning situation.

# Central Research Question

The central research question of Mission 004 is:

> **How does the frozen Recursive Reasoning Protocol behave when applied to a naturally occurring operational reasoning situation?**

The question is intentionally behavioral rather than confirmatory.

Mission 004 does not begin by asking whether the Protocol is valid.

It begins by observing what occurs when the Protocol is actually used.

---

# Mission Responsibility

Mission 004 is responsible for establishing an evidence-controlled relation between:

```text
Frozen Protocol
        +
Operational Reasoning Situation
        ↓
Protocol Application
        ↓
Behavior Observation
        ↓
Operational Evidence
```

The Mission should preserve sufficient information to determine:

- how operational entry occurs;
- whether Trigger / Re-entry can be identified;
- how Recursive Target and Depth are determined;
- how Preserve / Revise judgment behaves;
- how Restabilization is handled;
- whether Recursive Return occurs;
- where ambiguity or friction appears;
- whether responsibilities outside the Protocol are required;
- which Protocol mechanisms are not exercised;
- and whether unexpected operational behavior appears.

These are observation dimensions.

They are not predefined success criteria.

# Canonical Protocol Object

The Protocol Object used in Mission 004 is the frozen Canonical Protocol Candidate produced during Mission 002:

`08-protocol.md`

Mission 004 does not modify the Protocol Object before operational observation.

Mission 003 identified three principal downstream Revision Candidates:

```text
1. Materiality
2. Preserve → Restabilization explicitness
3. Recursive-return routing responsibility
```

These remain Revision Candidates.

They are not pre-authorized revisions.

They should not be repaired in advance of Mission 004 merely because they are already known.

If they become operationally relevant during observation, that relevance should be recorded as evidence.

---

# Observation Principle

Mission 004 follows the principle:

> **Observe behavior before interpreting success or failure.**

Operational use should therefore preserve distinctions among:

```text
What occurred
        ≠
How it is interpreted
        ≠
What it implies for the Protocol
        ≠
What future revision may be justified
```

Observation should precede assessment.

Assessment should precede downstream revision decisions.

---

# Natural Operational Situation

Mission 004 should not manufacture a reasoning problem solely to demonstrate the Protocol.

The operational case should arise from a genuine reasoning situation in which an existing reasoning state may require recursive reconsideration.

The exact entry conditions will be defined separately.

At Mission Definition level, the governing principle is:

```text
Protocol needs a test case
        ✗

Natural reasoning situation
creates a legitimate reason
for recursive reconsideration
        ✓
```

This protects the Mission from success-oriented case construction.

---

# Mission Non-Responsibilities

Mission 004 is not responsible for:

- proving that the Recursive Reasoning Protocol is generally valid;
- establishing independent validation;
- demonstrating empirical generalizability;
- redesigning the Protocol before observation;
- resolving all Revision Candidates identified in Mission 003;
- creating an artificial success case;
- benchmarking the Protocol against unrelated reasoning systems;
- conducting another Independent Examination;
- or producing a new canonical Protocol version.

Therefore:

```text
Operational Observation
        ≠
Protocol Validation

Successful Operational Use
        ≠
General Validity

Operational Friction
        ≠
Protocol Rejection

Single Case
        ≠
Generalizable Evidence
```

---

# Operational Failure Is Evidence

Mission 004 does not define Mission success as successful Protocol performance.

If the Protocol:

- becomes difficult to apply;
- produces ambiguity;
- requires responsibility not represented in the Protocol;
- cannot determine an operational route;
- generates unresolved recursive return;
- or becomes unusable within the selected case,

these outcomes should be preserved as operational evidence.

Accordingly:

```text
Smooth Behavior
        → Evidence

Qualified Behavior
        → Evidence

Friction
        → Evidence

Operational Failure
        → Evidence

Unexpected Behavior
        → Evidence
```

The Mission succeeds when operational behavior is validly observed and preserved, not when the Protocol produces a preferred outcome.

---

# Evidence Protection

Mission 004 should protect against at least the following risks:

- Confirmation Bias;
- Success-oriented Case Selection;
- Retrospective Case Reconstruction;
- Mid-observation Protocol Repair;
- Evidence–Interpretation Mixing;
- Known-Finding Search Bias;
- Validation Escalation;
- and Generalization from a Single Case.

Detailed protections will be defined in the Observation Boundary and Operational Procedure.

# Mission Success Condition

Mission 004 is considered successful when:

1. a legitimate operational reasoning situation is identified;
2. the frozen Protocol Object is applied without silent pre-observation modification;
3. operational behavior is preserved with sufficient fidelity;
4. observation is separated from interpretation;
5. friction, failure, non-use, and unexpected behavior remain recordable;
6. evidence boundaries are preserved;
7. and the resulting evidence status is explicitly determined.

Protocol success is not a Mission success requirement.

---

# Mission Failure Conditions

Mission 004 may fail as an observation if:

- the case is constructed primarily to produce a favorable result;
- the operational situation does not legitimately require recursive reasoning;
- the Protocol Object is silently modified during observation;
- observed behavior cannot be distinguished from retrospective reconstruction;
- evidence and interpretation become inseparable;
- known Mission 003 findings are intentionally forced into the case;
- or the resulting evidence cannot support a bounded operational judgment.

Mission failure should not automatically be interpreted as Protocol failure.
