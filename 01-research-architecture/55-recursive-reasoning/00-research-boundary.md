# Purpose

This document defines the research boundary for the Recursive Reasoning domain within the Research Architecture.

The purpose of this domain is to observe, externalize, evaluate, and progressively operationalize recursive reasoning structures that emerge through Human–AI collaborative research practice.

This domain does not begin from a predefined recursive reasoning methodology.

Instead, it preserves the developmental order through which recurring reasoning behavior is first observed, then extracted as a reusable pattern, and only later considered for protocol formation.

The governing principle is:

> **Observe before Design.**  
> **Extract before Generalize.**  
> **Protocol before Methodology.**

---

# Research Object

The primary research object is not recursion in the abstract.

The research object is the observable reasoning behavior through which previously externalized research or reasoning states are selectively revisited, re-evaluated, preserved, revised, and restabilized during ongoing Human–AI collaborative research.

Observed externalized states may include:

- repository assets;
- structural representations;
- analytical outputs;
- validation records;
- research architectures;
- paper architectures;
- process models;
- role interpretations;
- conversation-derived reasoning states.

Repository assets are therefore treated as potential components of an external reasoning environment rather than merely as static documentation.

However, repository revisiting alone does not define recursive reasoning.

The broader object of study is the conditional re-observation and re-evaluation of previously externalized reasoning states.

---

# Developmental Boundary

Recursive Reasoning research follows the developmental sequence:

**Observation**

↓

**Operational Pattern**

↓

**Protocol Candidate**

↓

**Validated Protocol**

↓

**Methodology**

These stages must remain distinct.

Evidence established at one stage does not automatically justify promotion to the next stage.

In particular:

- an observed behavior is not automatically a reusable pattern;
- a reusable pattern is not automatically a protocol;
- a protocol candidate is not automatically a validated protocol;
- a validated protocol is not automatically a methodology.

Advancement between stages requires explicit evidence and assessment.

# Core Boundary Principle

The research must distinguish between:

> **what was observed**

and

> **what should be instructed**

Mission 001 primarily addressed the first question.

Mission 002 begins to investigate the second.

Observed recursive behavior must not be converted mechanically into mandatory procedural instructions.

For example, the observation that research roles sometimes revisit previous repository assets does not justify an instruction requiring repository revisiting at every reasoning stage.

Similarly, observed recursive reasoning includes not only re-entry and revision, but also:

- deciding not to re-enter;
- preserving previous reasoning;
- limiting recursive depth;
- restabilizing the reasoning state;
- stopping or freezing when sufficient stability has been reached.

Any future protocol candidate must preserve these selective characteristics.

## Out of Scope

This research domain is not responsible for:

- conducting the original research tasks of individual specialized roles;
- producing Structural Representations;
- conducting Comparative Observation as its primary mission;
- designing repository architectures;
- redesigning Research Architecture;
- creating general theories of intelligence;
- defining universal AI reasoning architectures;
- assuming that recursive reasoning always improves reasoning quality;
- prematurely defining a Recursive Reasoning Methodology.

Evidence from other research roles may be observed here, but their original research responsibilities remain outside this domain.

# Relationship to Repository Assets

Repository assets may serve three distinct functions within this research domain.

## 1. Evidence

Existing assets may provide evidence of previous reasoning states and reasoning transitions.

## 2. Externalized Reasoning State

A repository asset may preserve a stabilized reasoning state that can later become an object of re-observation.

## 3. Re-entry Resource

A previously externalized state may be selectively revisited when later reasoning produces a relevant trigger.

These functions must not be confused with a requirement to revisit repositories continuously.

Repository re-entry remains conditional.

# Evidence Discipline

All major claims in this research domain should preserve their epistemic status.

Recommended status distinctions include:

- Observed;
- Emerging Observation;
- Provisional;
- Evidence-Supported;
- Protocol Candidate;
- Validated.

Terminology should not imply a stronger maturity level than the available evidence supports.

When new evidence conflicts with an existing stable asset, the existing asset should not be silently rewritten.

The conflict should first be recorded as a validation issue or development event and then evaluated explicitly.

---

# Recursive Revision Boundary

Existing research assets remain revisable.

However, recursive revision does not mean continuous modification.

A previously stabilized asset should be reopened only when a relevant trigger justifies re-evaluation.

Possible triggers include:

- new evidence;
- structural mismatch;
- detected gaps;
- validation requirements;
- Human intervention;
- perspective shifts;
- post-stabilization re-observation.

Re-entry should remain selective.

The required depth of re-evaluation should be determined before broader restructuring is attempted.

Unaffected stable layers should be preserved whenever the evidence permits.

---

# Stop and Freeze Principle

Recursive reasoning requires explicit stopping conditions.

More recursion does not automatically imply better reasoning.

Research assets may be frozen when:

- available evidence is sufficient for the current research question;
- validation objectives have been completed;
- the relevant reasoning state has stabilized;
- further recursion would exceed the current mission scope;
- the next question belongs to a different developmental stage or mission.

Freeze does not mean permanent immutability.

It means that the asset remains stable until a justified re-entry condition emerges.
