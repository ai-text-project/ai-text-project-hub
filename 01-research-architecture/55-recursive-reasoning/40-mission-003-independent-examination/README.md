# Overview

Mission 003 conducts an independent examination of the Recursive Reasoning Protocol Candidate formed during Mission 002.

Mission 002 developed and internally examined the Protocol Candidate.

Mission 003 introduces a different evidential relationship.

Its central question is:

> **What happens when the completed Protocol Candidate is examined by a reasoning instance operationally separated from the process that formed it?**

The purpose is not to repeat Mission 002.

The purpose is not to revise the Protocol during examination.

The purpose is not to assume that independent examination produces validation.

Instead, Mission 003 creates a controlled separation between:

> **Protocol Formation**

and:

> **Protocol Examination**

and uses that separation to generate a materially new judgment relation.

# Mission Objective

The objective of Mission 003 is to determine whether the Recursive Reasoning Protocol Candidate remains coherent and defensible when examined independently of its formation trajectory.

The examination addresses:

- overall Protocol architecture
- differentiation of principal mechanisms
- responsibility boundaries
- operational principles and invariants
- failure protections
- Trigger and Re-entry logic
- Target and Depth logic
- Preservation and Revision logic
- Restabilization and Exit logic
- Human–AI Coupling
- internal contradiction
- omission
- ambiguity
- redundancy
- unsupported structural dependency
- overall procedural usability
- legitimate evidence claims

The examination permits:

- PRESERVE
- REVISE
- QUALIFY
- REJECT
- INSUFFICIENT INFORMATION
- UNEXPECTED OBSERVATION

No outcome is precommitted.

> **a materially new judgment relation**


Necessary Object Information is preserved.
