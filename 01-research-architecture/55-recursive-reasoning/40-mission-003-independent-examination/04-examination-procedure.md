**Research Area:** Recursive Reasoning  
**Mission:** 003 — Independent Examination  
**Asset:** 04 — Examination Procedure  
**Status:** Current Baseline  
**Upstream Assets:** `01-examination-boundary.md`, `02-independent-instance-protocol.md`, `03-examination-materials.md`

# Purpose

This document defines the operational procedure through which the Independent Examination of the Recursive Reasoning Protocol Candidate is conducted.

The preceding Mission 003 assets established:

```text
01 — Examination Boundary
        ↓
What must remain protected

02 — Independent Instance Protocol
        ↓
What operational separation must exist

03 — Examination Materials
        ↓
What information should be exposed

04 — Examination Procedure
        ↓
How the examination should occur
```

This asset is the final pre-examination design asset before the Examination Gate.

Its central question is:

> **How should the independent examination be conducted so that the material package is presented without reintroducing formation influence and the resulting judgment can be preserved as usable evidence?**

The objective is not to guarantee a favorable examination outcome.

The objective is to establish a procedure capable of producing a materially new and traceable judgment relation.

---

# Procedure Responsibility

The responsibility of this asset is:

```text
04 — Examination Procedure
        ↓
Operationalize the
Independent Examination
```

This includes:

- establishing the examination environment;
- presenting the approved Examination Material Package;
- initiating independent examination;
- controlling clarification;
- limiting Human intervention;
- preserving the resulting judgment;
- and recording procedural events relevant to later evidence interpretation.

This asset does not:

- redefine the Mission;
- redesign the Protocol Candidate;
- redefine the Examination Boundary;
- redesign the Examination Material Package;
- interpret the examination result;
- compare the result with Mission 002;
- or assign final Validation Status.

Those responsibilities remain elsewhere in the Mission architecture.

# Core Procedure

The Current Baseline defines five primary procedural stages:

```text
Initialize
    ↓
Expose
    ↓
Examine
    ↓
Clarify if Necessary
    ↓
Capture
```

More explicitly:

```text
Fresh Examination State
        ↓
Controlled Material Exposure
        ↓
Independent Examination
        ↓
Optional Controlled Clarification
        ↓
Raw Judgment Capture
        ↓
========================
    EVIDENCE CREATED
========================
```

Each stage has a distinct responsibility.

---

# Stage 1 — Initialize

The first stage establishes the Independent Examination Environment.

The purpose is not yet to evaluate the Protocol Candidate.

The purpose is to establish a reasoning state sufficiently separated from the formation environment.

The initialization requirement is:

```text
Fresh Reasoning State
        +
No Active Mission 002
Formation Trajectory
        ↓
Independent Examination Environment
```

The operational implementation may involve:

- a fresh conversation;
- a fresh reasoning instance;
- another model instance;
- or another sufficiently separated reasoning environment.

However:

```text
Fresh Chat
        ≠
Independence by Definition
```

and:

```text
Different Model
        ≠
Independence by Definition
```

The relevant requirement remains functional separation from the active Protocol formation process.

---

# Initialization Condition

Before material exposure begins, the examination environment should satisfy:

```text
Active Mission 002 reasoning state?
        NO

Prior developmental dialogue loaded?
        NO by default

Prior Counter-test history loaded?
        NO by default

Expected outcome supplied?
        NO

Canonical examination object available?
        YES

Examination responsibility identifiable?
        YES
```

If these conditions cannot reasonably be established, the examination should not proceed as an Independent Examination without qualification.

---

# Initialization Is Not Formation Reconstruction

The Independent Instance should not be initialized by reconstructing the full history of Recursive Reasoning research.

For example:

```text
Mission 001 history
        ↓
Mission 002 formation
        ↓
Counter-tests
        ↓
Preservation rationales
        ↓
Now independently examine it
```

would reproduce much of the formation environment before requesting independence.

The preferred sequence is instead:

```text
Fresh Reasoning State
        ↓
Necessary Examination Context
        ↓
Canonical Protocol Object
        ↓
Independent Examination
```

---

# Stage 2 — Expose

Once the examination environment has been established, the Examination Material Package defined in `03-examination-materials.md` is presented.

The package consists of:

```text
Examination Mandate
        +
Necessary Object Context
        +
Canonical Protocol Object
        +
Evidence Boundary
```

04 does not redesign these materials.

Its responsibility is to expose them without introducing unnecessary formation-conditioning information.

---

# Controlled Material Exposure

Material exposure should preserve:

```text
Object Validity
```

while maintaining:

```text
Context Control
```

The exposure sequence should therefore avoid commentary such as:

```text
"This structure survived several
strong Counter-tests."
```

```text
"We believe this is the
most robust version."
```

```text
"The important point is
that you should preserve X."
```

Such statements may introduce prior evaluation into the examination environment.

---

# Material Presentation Principle

The presentation principle is:

> **Present the examination object and necessary context without presenting the prior argument for why the object should be accepted.**

Thus:

```text
Object
        ✓

Necessary Context
        ✓

Task Responsibility
        ✓

Evidence Boundary
        ✓

Prior Defense
        ✗

Expected Judgment
        ✗
```

---

# Stage 3 — Examine

After material exposure, the Independent Instance performs the examination.

The examination should permit materially different outcomes.

The allowed judgment space includes:

```text
Preserve

Revise

Qualify

Reject

Unable to Reach
a Justified Judgment

Unexpected Observation
```

No one of these outcomes is defined in advance as success.

---

# Judgment Freedom

The examination procedure must not encode:

```text
Agreement = Success
```

or:

```text
Disagreement = Failure
```

The purpose is evidence production, not confirmation.

Therefore:

```text
Independent Judgment
        ↓
may converge with
Mission 002

OR

Independent Judgment
        ↓
may diverge from
Mission 002
```

Both may be evidentially useful.

---

# Examination Prompt Principle

The examination prompt should be sufficient to establish:

```text
Task Identity
        +
Object Identity
        +
Judgment Freedom
        +
Evidence Boundary
```

without embedding a preferred conclusion.

A minimal prompt structure may therefore resemble:

```text
You are conducting an independent
examination of the supplied
Recursive Reasoning Protocol Candidate.

Examine the supplied object on its
presented structure and evidence boundary.

Identify material reasons to preserve,
revise, qualify, or reject its structure.

Do not assume that prior internal
examination establishes validity.

If necessary information is missing,
identify the missing information rather
than inferring the intended answer.
```

This represents the procedural structure of the prompt.

Minor wording adjustments may be made during operational preparation provided they do not change the Examination Boundary or introduce Judgment Steering.

---

# Examination Is Not Criticism by Default

The term:

```text
Independent Examination
```

must not be operationalized as:

```text
Find as many weaknesses
as possible
```

because this would introduce a negative Task-Framing Bias.

Likewise, it must not be operationalized as:

```text
Confirm whether the
Protocol is robust
```

because this would introduce positive confirmation framing.

The examination should instead permit:

```text
Preservation
        │
Revision
        │
Qualification
        │
Rejection
        │
Unexpected Observation
```

without pre-ranking these outcomes.

---

# Task-Framing Bias

Task framing itself may influence the behavior of the Independent Instance.

Two major directions are possible.

## Negative Bias

```text
"Examine"
        ↓
Interpreted as
"Criticize"
        ↓
Artificial weakness seeking
```

## Positive Bias

```text
"Protocol Candidate"
        ↓
Assumed prior quality
        ↓
Artificial preservation tendency
```

The procedure cannot eliminate all framing effects.

It can, however, avoid explicitly privileging either direction.

This is why the judgment space must remain symmetric.

---

# Independent Reasoning

During the examination stage, the Independent Instance should be allowed to determine:

- what structures appear material;
- what weaknesses appear material;
- what relationships require examination;
- whether the Protocol appears coherent;
- whether important conditions are missing;
- whether some structures should be preserved;
- and whether unexpected problems or alternatives exist.

The Human should not continuously redirect this reasoning toward the Mission 002 development path.

---

# Light Output Structure

The Independent Instance may be given a light output structure to improve evidence preservation.

A suitable structure may include:

```text
Overall Judgment

Primary Reasons

Material Weaknesses or Limitations

Missing Information

Unexpected Observations
```

This structure should remain light.

It should not become a detailed analytic taxonomy that predetermines what the Independent Instance is expected to find.

---

# Why Strong Output Schemas Are Avoided

A strongly structured response schema may improve comparability.

However:

```text
Human-designed categories
        ↓
Independent search space narrowed
        ↓
Potentially missed
unexpected observations
```

Mission 003 therefore prioritizes open examination over maximal output standardization.

The initial Independent Examination should preserve room for observations not anticipated by the existing research architecture.

---

# Stage 4 — Clarify if Necessary

Clarification is permitted when the Independent Instance identifies information that is materially necessary to continue examination.

Clarification is not automatic.

It is triggered by a detected information gap.

```text
Independent Examination
        ↓
Information Gap Detected
        ↓
Clarification Request
```

The request then passes through a Clarification Gate.

---

# Clarification Gate

The Clarification Gate asks:

> **Is the requested information materially necessary for Object Validity?**

Conceptually:

```text
Clarification Request
        ↓
Object Necessity Test
        │
    ┌───┴────┐
    ▼        ▼
Necessary   Not Necessary
    │        │
    ▼        ▼
Provide     Do Not Add
Minimal     Formation Context
Neutral
Context
```

The preferred clarification is:

```text
Minimal
        +
Object-directed
        +
Formation-neutral
        +
Traceable
```

---

# Clarification Is Not Formation Reconstruction

A clarification response should not automatically contain:

- Mission 002 developmental history;
- previous Counter-tests;
- rejected alternatives;
- preservation rationales;
- expected interpretation;
- or prior conclusions.

For example, a request such as:

```text
"What does this term mean?"
```

should not automatically receive:

```text
"During Mission 002 we originally
considered three alternatives,
then rejected two because..."
```

The first requires object clarification.

The second reconstructs formation reasoning.

---

# Human Mediation

The Human may mediate clarification.

However, the Human's responsibility is constrained.

```text
Human
   │
   ├─ Establish Environment
   │        ✓
   │
   ├─ Transfer Fixed Materials
   │        ✓
   │
   ├─ Provide Necessary
   │  Neutral Clarification
   │        ✓
   │
   ├─ Preserve Examination Output
   │        ✓
   │
   └─ Shape Independent Judgment
            ✗
```

Human participation is therefore compatible with Independent Examination only when Judgment Formation remains outside the Human's procedural responsibility.

---

# Mid-Examination Formation Leakage

Formation Leakage may occur after a clean initialization.

For example:

```text
Fresh Examination State
        ✓

Controlled Initial Exposure
        ✓

Independent Examination Begins
        ✓

Human explains:
"During Mission 002..."
        ↓
Formation History Reintroduced
```

This means Context Control must continue throughout the examination.

It is not merely an initialization requirement.

---

# Additional Exposure Logging

Any material added after initial exposure should be recorded.

At minimum:

```text
What was requested?

What was provided?

Why was it provided?

At what point in the
examination was it provided?
```

This preserves later interpretability of the resulting evidence.

Thus:

```text
Additional Exposure
        ↓
LOG
```

is a procedural requirement.

---

# Clarification Deadlock

A special case occurs when the Independent Instance requires information that cannot be provided without materially reconstructing Formation History.

For example:

```text
Clarification Required
        ↓
Object Context unavailable
without Formation History
        ↓
Context Control Conflict
```

The procedure should not force resolution.

The examination may legitimately stop.

This leads to an important procedural principle:

> **Non-completion is a legitimate Independent Examination result.**

---

# Non-Completion as Evidence

If the Independent Instance cannot meaningfully examine the Protocol Candidate without substantial Formation History, that observation may itself indicate:

```text
Protocol Self-containment Issue

Material Sufficiency Issue

Hidden Context Dependency

Terminological Ambiguity

Representation Problem
```

Therefore:

```text
Examination Non-completion
        ≠
Automatic Procedure Failure
```

It may instead constitute evidence.

The reason for non-completion must be preserved.

---

# Unable to Reach a Justified Judgment

The Independent Instance is not required to produce a forced Preserve / Revise / Reject judgment.

A legitimate outcome is:

```text
Unable to Reach
a Justified Judgment
```

provided that the reasons are preserved.

Forcing a substantive judgment despite insufficient information would weaken the evidential value of the examination.

---

# Stage 5 — Capture

Once the Independent Instance has completed the examination, or reached a legitimate non-completion condition, the resulting output is captured.

The primary principle is:

```text
Capture
        ≠
Interpret
```

The first responsibility is preservation.

Interpretation belongs downstream.

---

# Raw Judgment Capture

The examination output should first be preserved as produced.

Conceptually:

```text
Independent Examination
        ↓
Raw Examination Output
        ↓
Preserve
        ↓
05 — Examination Results
```

The output should not first pass through:

```text
Human Editing
        ↓
Research-friendly Rewrite
        ↓
Evidence Record
```

because this would mix evidence production with interpretation.

---

# Preservation of Context

Raw Judgment Capture should preserve, where relevant:

```text
Initial Examination Output

Clarification Requests

Clarification Responses

Additional Material Exposure

Non-completion Conditions

Unexpected Observations
```

This enables later assessment of how the judgment was produced.

---

# Evidence Production versus Evidence Interpretation

Mission 003 maintains the separation:

```text
04
Evidence Production Procedure
        ↓
05
Examination Results
        ↓
06
Comparative Assessment
        ↓
07
Validation Status
```

04 should not anticipate the work of 06 or 07.

In particular:

```text
Agreement
        ≠
Automatic Validation
```

and:

```text
Disagreement
        ≠
Automatic Refutation
```

# Pre-Execution Failure Test

Before externalization, the Current Procedure was subjected to a Pre-Execution Failure Test.

The test asked:

> **Can the Independent Examination still fail even when the procedure is followed correctly?**

The answer was:

```text
YES
```

Several legitimate failure or difficulty conditions remain possible.

The objective of the procedure is therefore not to eliminate every failure.

The stronger requirement is:

> **When examination difficulty or failure occurs, can its source remain sufficiently identifiable to preserve evidential value?**

---

# Failure Scenario 1 — Task-Framing Bias

Even with correct materials and a fresh reasoning state:

```text
Independent Examination
        ↓
may be interpreted as
"Criticism"
```

or:

```text
Protocol Candidate
        ↓
may create an assumption
of prior quality
```

The procedure mitigates this by preserving symmetric Judgment Freedom.

No additional procedural stage is required.

---

# Failure Scenario 2 — Undetected Object Misinterpretation

An Independent Instance may misunderstand the object without recognizing that clarification is needed.

```text
Correct Materials
        ↓
Misinterpretation
        ↓
No Clarification Request
        ↓
Independent Judgment
```

This risk cannot be eliminated through continuous Human correction without threatening Judgment Independence.

It therefore remains a residual Evidence Quality Risk.

The Raw Examination Output must preserve enough reasoning context for later assessment of Object Fidelity.

---

# Failure Scenario 3 — Clarification Deadlock

The Independent Instance may require context that cannot be supplied without substantial Formation History.

The legitimate responses include:

```text
Controlled additional context
```

or, where separation cannot reasonably be maintained:

```text
Examination Non-completion
```

Non-completion remains evidentially relevant.

---

# Failure Scenario 4 — Human Intervention Leakage

Human explanation may unintentionally reintroduce formation reasoning.

The procedure therefore requires:

```text
Minimal Neutral Clarification
        +
Additional Exposure Logging
```

Context Control remains active throughout the examination.

---

# Failure Scenario 5 — No Substantive Judgment

The Independent Instance may conclude that available information is insufficient.

This is not automatically a failed examination.

The inability to reach a justified judgment may reveal a material dependency or insufficiency.

The result should therefore be preserved rather than overridden.

---

# Failure Scenario 6 — Unstructured Output

A completely open response may produce difficult-to-interpret evidence.

A highly structured response may over-direct the examination.

The Current Baseline therefore adopts:

```text
Light Output Structure
```

rather than:

```text
Rigid Judgment Schema
```

---

# Failure Scenario 7 — Near-Perfect Agreement

Near-perfect agreement with Mission 002 does not itself establish Independent Validation.

Possible explanations include:

```text
Independent Convergence

Shared Model Tendencies

Common Interpretive Bias

Material Framing Effects
```

These possibilities belong to downstream Comparative Assessment.

---

# Failure Scenario 8 — Procedure-Induced Context

The examination necessarily occurs inside an intentionally constructed procedure.

Therefore the resulting evidence supports claims about:

```text
Judgment produced
under the defined
Independent Examination Environment
```

It does not automatically support universal claims about how all AI instances would reason under arbitrary conditions.

This limitation belongs to the Evidence Boundary.
