# Information Architecture

## Purpose

This section defines the Information Architecture of the AI Text Project Hub.

Whereas the Research Architecture describes how the Research Program itself is organized, the Information Architecture defines how research assets are organized, classified, recorded, and maintained throughout the Repository.

Its objective is to provide a stable organizational framework that supports long-term consistency as the Research Program continues to evolve.

---

# Scope

The Information Architecture focuses on the organizational structure of research assets rather than on the research content itself.

Typical topics include:

- Information Domains
- Recording Objects
- Placement Principles
- Folder Responsibilities
- Organizational Consistency
- Information Architecture Evolution

The purpose of this section is not to define research theories or methodologies.

Instead, it defines the organizational principles governing how research knowledge is preserved within the Repository.

# Relationship to Other Architectural Layers

The Information Architecture complements several other architectural layers maintained within the Research Program.

Research Architecture

- Defines the structural organization of the Research Program.

Operational Architecture

- Defines operational workflows and reusable operational assets.

Repository Topology

- Defines relationships among repositories.

Research Space Topology

- Defines the internal structural organization of the Research Space.

Information Architecture

- Defines how research knowledge is organized, classified, and maintained across these architectural layers.

These architectural layers serve complementary responsibilities while collectively supporting the long-term development of the Research Program.

---

# Design Philosophy

The Information Architecture is designed around organizational responsibility rather than document type.

Research assets are therefore classified according to their primary organizational role rather than their creation date or file format.

This approach enables the Repository to remain consistent as new research assets, methodologies, operational packages, and architectural documents continue to emerge.
