# Observation Methodology Boundary Expansion

## Purpose

This document records an observed expansion of the apparent application boundary of the Observation Methodology.

The objective is not to generalize the methodology.

The objective is to document an observed transition requiring future validation.

---

# Background

The Observation Methodology was initially developed through long-horizon dialogue observation, repository development, structural representation, and research-space formation.

Throughout these activities, GitHub repositories and dialogue records served as the primary observation environment.

At that stage, it remained unclear whether the methodology depended upon those environments or merely emerged within them.

---

# Trigger

The development of the following activities introduced a different observation target.

Examples include:

- Research Collaboration Opportunity Mapping
- Organization Observation
- Career Observation
- Market Observation

Unlike previous observations, these activities operate primarily on publicly observable external systems rather than internally constructed research repositories.

---

# Observation

Despite the change of observation source, the apparent observation process remained largely unchanged.

The observed sequence continues to appear as:

Observation

↓

Pattern Recognition

↓

Structure Discovery

↓

Structural Correspondence

↓

Interpretation

↓

Decision

No substantial modification of the process itself has yet been observed.

Only the observation source appears to have changed.

---

# Preliminary Interpretation

A possible interpretation is that GitHub repositories were not the methodology itself.

Instead, they may have been the first environment in which the Observation Methodology became sufficiently visible for sustained development.

If correct, the methodology may depend more strongly upon the observation process than upon any particular observation source.

This interpretation remains provisional.

---

# Current Evidence

Observed within:

- Dialogue Observation
- Repository Observation
- Research Space Observation
- Organization Observation
- Career Observation
- Early-stage Market Observation

These observations currently suggest consistency of the process across multiple observation domains.

The evidence remains insufficient for broader generalization.

---

# Open Questions

Several questions remain unresolved.

- Does the methodology remain stable across fundamentally different observation domains?

- Which components are source-independent?

- Which components remain domain-specific?

- Is "Observation → Structure Discovery → Structural Correspondence" the stable core of the methodology, or only one currently observed implementation?

These questions require additional empirical observation.

---

# Current Position

This document records a methodological boundary observation.

It does not claim methodological universality.

It records the first observed indication that the Observation Methodology may extend beyond its original repository-centered environment.

Future observations should determine whether this represents genuine methodological generality or only a temporary similarity across domains.
