# Structural Representation Methodology Stabilization Assessment

**Methodological Maturation Record:** MM-007

**Status:** Provisional

---

# Purpose

This document assesses the current stabilization of the Structural Representation Methodology following its emergence, intentional application, and methodological review.

Its objective is not to introduce a new methodology.

Instead, it evaluates which methodological components can now be regarded as operationally stable based upon accumulated observational evidence.

Accordingly, this document represents the first stabilization assessment of the Structural Representation Methodology.

---

# Background

The Structural Representation Methodology has progressed through multiple developmental stages.

Initially, the methodology emerged naturally during the construction of the ACIM Structural Representation Repository.

Subsequently, the methodology was intentionally applied to the SOAR cognitive architecture.

Following repository completion, methodological review identified additional refinements and clarified the operational responsibilities of each structural asset.

The present assessment evaluates the cumulative evidence produced through these completed stages.

# Scope of Assessment

The assessment considers evidence obtained from:

- ACIM Structural Representation Repository

- SOAR Structural Representation Repository
