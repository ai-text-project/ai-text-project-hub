# Methodological Evolution

**Methodological Evolution Version:** v0.1 (Provisional)

---

# Purpose

This directory records the provisional historical development of observational methodologies that have gradually emerged throughout the Research Program.

The objective is not to establish a unified methodological framework.

Instead, this directory documents recurring methodological developments observed across independently conducted research activities.

Accordingly, the present directory should be interpreted as a historical record of methodological emergence rather than a finalized methodological specification.

---

# Background

Several research projects were originally developed independently for different research objectives.

These projects were not intentionally designed to share a common observational methodology.

However, historical review has gradually revealed that similar organizational processes repeatedly emerged during structural observation and comparative analysis.

These recurring observations motivated the creation of the present Methodological Evolution series.
