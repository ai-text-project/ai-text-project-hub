# Purpose

This document examines whether the four source domains independently observed in `02-source-observation.md` exhibit meaningful **Structural Correspondence**.

The four source domains are:

```text
Source A — ACIM
Source B — Primitive Triangle Research
Source C — Bashar Transcription
Source D — Human–AI Dialogue Observation
```

This stage permits cross-source comparison for the first time.

However, the purpose is not to construct a unified theory.

The purpose is to determine:

1. which relations or transformations appear structurally correspondent;
2. which correspond only partially;
3. which do not correspond;
4. which remain undetermined;
5. and whether any cross-source structural pattern is sufficiently stable to justify further investigation.

The comparison is restricted to the observations already recorded in `02-source-observation.md`.

No additional source material is introduced at this stage.

---

# 1. Governing Principle

The governing principle is:

> **Compare relations and transformations before comparing concepts.**

Accordingly, this inquiry does not begin with mappings such as:

```text
ACIM Concept X
        =
Bashar Concept Y
        =
Human–AI Concept Z
```

Instead, it asks whether independently observed systems exhibit comparable structural relations such as:

```text
Prior Condition
      ↓
Structural Change
      ↓
Organization
      ↓
Projection / Expression
      ↓
Perception
      ↓
Experience
      ↓
Feedback / Recurrence
```

The presence of similar relational sequences does not establish that the underlying concepts, mechanisms, or ontologies are identical.

---

# 2. Correspondence Categories

Four categories are used throughout this document.

## Correspondence

A sufficiently similar relational or transformational structure is present in the compared sources.

This does not imply conceptual or ontological identity.

---

## Partial Correspondence

Some structural features correspond, while important elements, directionality, mechanism, or explanatory function differ.

---

## Non-Correspondence

The sources organize the relevant phenomenon differently enough that structural correspondence should not be asserted.

---

## Undetermined

The available source observation is insufficient to support either correspondence or non-correspondence.
