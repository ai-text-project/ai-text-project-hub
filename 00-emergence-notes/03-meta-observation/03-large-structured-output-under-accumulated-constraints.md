**Status:** Provisional Meta-Observation

# Purpose

This document records an unexpected behavior observed during the development of the:

`00-emergence-notes/04-emerging-inquiries/001-human-ai-experience-formation/`

inquiry package.

The observation concerns the relationship among:

```text
Dialogue Volume
Structural Accumulation
Boundary Preservation
Recursive Re-entry
Constraint Accumulation
Structured Output Expansion
```

During the inquiry, several unusually large Markdown documents were generated despite the immediately preceding dialogue itself being comparatively limited.

The purpose of this note is not to explain why this occurred.

The purpose is to preserve the observation before it is absorbed into an explanatory framework.

In particular, this document does **not** assume that:

- Recursive Reasoning caused the behavior;
- model-internal recursion occurred;
- accumulated constraints caused output expansion;
- output length indicates reasoning depth;
- output length indicates research quality;
- or the observed behavior generalizes to other AI instances or tasks.

The phenomenon is recorded first.

Explanation remains provisional.


