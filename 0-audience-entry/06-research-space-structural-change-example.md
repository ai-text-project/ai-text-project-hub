# Observable Changes

Between the two snapshots, several repository-level structural changes can be directly observed.

### 1. Audience Entry appeared as an independent structure

A dedicated `0-audience-entry/` area became visible.

It provides different entry paths for:

* enterprise and industry visitors;
* academic and research visitors;
* dialogue and collaboration;
* general research overview.

### 2. Architecture Integration became explicit

`0-architecture-layer-integration/` appeared as a separate area for relating multiple architectural responsibilities.

### 3. Information Architecture became independently represented

`04-information-architecture/` became a dedicated structural area concerned with information domains, recording objects, placement principles, folder responsibilities, and organizational consistency.

### 4. Research Architecture became more functionally differentiated

Research activities that had previously appeared as a relatively flat collection of development areas were reorganized into explicitly separated functional domains, including:

* Core Architecture
* Paper Assembly
* Presentation Audience
* Presentation Development
* Harvest Development
* Recursive Reasoning
* Protocols
* Methodologies

### 5. The change is not only an increase in files

The comparison suggests an observable change in how different research responsibilities are represented and separated within the Repository.

This example therefore illustrates **structural differentiation**, not merely asset accumulation.

# Evidence Boundary

This comparison shows what changed in the observable GitHub Repository structure between the two selected historical states.

* why these structural changes occurred;
