# Research Space Evolution

**Status: Provisional / Illustrative**

## Purpose

This page provides an illustrative view of how the Research Space may be observed as a structure that changes over time.

The purpose is not to claim that every repository change represents research evolution.

Instead, GitHub history can provide observable structural evidence showing how folders, documents, research functions, and navigation structures appeared, differentiated, or were reorganized over time.

---

## Research Space as a Changing Structure

The current Research Space should not necessarily be understood as a structure that existed in its present form from the beginning.

One possible way to observe its formation is to compare repository structures at different points in time.

For example:

Time A
↓
Repository Structure A

↓

Human–AI Dialogue
Research Activity
New Questions
New Roles
New Methods

↓

Time B
↓
Repository Structure B

The difference between Structure A and Structure B can provide observable evidence of structural change.

However, structural change alone does not establish why the change occurred.

The formation process should therefore be examined separately using dialogue records, research records, and other available evidence.

# Observation Protocol — Provisional

A possible observation procedure is:

1. Select representative historical points in the repository.

2. Identify the corresponding Git commits.

3. Reconstruct the repository tree at each selected point.

4. Compare structural differences between the trees.

5. Record observable changes such as:

   - folder creation;
   - functional differentiation;
   - new architectural layers;
   - navigation changes;
   - consolidation or separation of research functions.

6. Distinguish structural observation from interpretation.

7. Where necessary, compare the structural changes with dialogue records or other research evidence.

8. Only after this comparison, evaluate whether the observed change can reasonably be described as Research Space formation or evolution.

---

## Evidence Boundary

GitHub history can show:

- what structure existed at a particular point;
- what was added, removed, renamed, or reorganized;
- when observable structural changes occurred.

GitHub history alone cannot establish:

- why a structural change occurred;
- whether the change emerged through Human–AI Dialogue;
- whether the change represents methodological or conceptual development.

These questions require additional evidence.
