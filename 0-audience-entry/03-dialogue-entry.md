# 日本語

## 対話から、次の探索へ

AI Text Projectにご関心をお持ちいただき、ありがとうございます。

この研究では、研究成果を一方向に説明するだけではなく、
異なる経験や視点を持つ方々との対話を大切にしています。

ここから先に、決められた読み方はありません。

ご自身の関心に近い入口から、自由に研究空間をご覧ください。

## 対話について

この研究空間をご覧いただく中で、

- 研究について質問してみたい
- ご自身の経験や視点と比較してみたい
- Human–AI Collaborationについて意見交換してみたい
- もう少し対話を続けてみたい

と感じられましたら、お気軽にご連絡ください。

**Contact:** ai.text.project@gmail.com

対話の先に何があるかを、あらかじめ決めているわけではありません。

異なる経験や視点を持ち寄りながら、
そこからどのような問いや可能性が生まれるかを
一緒に探索できればと考えています。

# English

## From Dialogue to Further Exploration

Thank you for your interest in the AI Text Project.

This research values dialogue with people who bring
different experiences and perspectives,
rather than simply presenting research findings in one direction.

There is no required path from here.

Please explore the research space through whichever entry
best matches your interests.

---

## Understand the Research Program

If you would first like to understand
the overall research program and its structure,
you can begin with the Research Overview.

**→ [View the Research Overview](./04-research-overview.md)**

---

## Explore the Enterprise / Practice Perspective

If you are interested in AI research,
Human–AI Collaboration,
enterprise AI practice,
or connections between research and practice,
you can explore the Enterprise Research Experience.

**→ [View the Enterprise Research Experience](../01-research-architecture/30-presentation-audience/README.md)**

---

## Explore the Research Space

If you would like to explore Dialogue-Phase Reasoning
and related research assets more freely,
you can continue through the Research Entry.

**→ [Explore the Research Space](https://github.com/ai-text-project/dialogue-phase-reasoning/tree/main/00-research-entry)**

---

## Continue the Dialogue

As you explore the research space, you may find that you would like to:

- ask a question about the research,
- compare it with your own experience or perspective,
- exchange views on Human–AI Collaboration, or
- simply continue the conversation.

If so, please feel free to get in touch.

**Contact:** ai.text.project@gmail.com

There is no predetermined outcome expected from the dialogue.

The purpose is simply to bring different experiences
and perspectives into conversation and explore
what questions or possibilities may emerge.
